About Latest News Updates

Latest News Updates brings Pakistan and world news together in one place. Live headlines from Pakistani and international channels update every hour, and each headline links to the full report on the original website.

How our stories are written

Twice a day, at about 8 AM and 8 PM Pakistan time, we publish short news stories written with AI assistance. Each story uses only facts that appear in the reports it lists as sources, names who reported each claim, and links to every source. Stories that contain a number not found in their sources are rejected automatically before publishing.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, please tell us. We correct errors as quickly as we can.

Ownership of headlines

Headlines, snippets and links belong to their original publishers. We show only titles, short snippets and links, never full copied articles.
