Pope Leo XIV Warns Against Austerity During Argentina Visit

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 1:38 AM PKT

Pope Leo XIV conducted a major mass in Buenos Aires that drew an estimated 2.5 million attendees, making it his largest gathering yet, according to DW. The pontiff's appearance comes in the midst of a 12-day tour across South America, Euronews reported.

During his trip, the Pope visited the Cottolengo Don Orione in Claypole, a facility supporting over 400 individuals, Euronews reported. Amid cuts enacted by President Javier Milei affecting disability care and broader social programs, DW and Euronews noted that the Pope urged the protection of society's most vulnerable populations, asserting that national progress should be measured by the treatment of those with the greatest needs.
