Former Afghan Army Member Detained in Peshawar Over Terror Allegations

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

Law enforcement agencies and security forces have arrested an individual previously serving in the Afghan National Army (ANA) for alleged involvement in terrorist operations within Pakistan, Dawn reported.

The arrest was disclosed on Wednesday during a media briefing led by Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Additional Inspector General Abbas Ahsan, according to Dawn.
