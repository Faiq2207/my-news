PCB announces central contracts for 38 men's cricketers under revamped structure

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:56 PM PKT

According to Dawn, Samaa, and Dunya News, the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) announced central contracts for 38 men's cricketers under a revamped, format-based structure that places greater emphasis on specialised roles.

Dawn and Express Tribune reported that former captain Mohammad Rizwan, Shan Masood, and Saim Ayub were among the notable omissions from the list.
