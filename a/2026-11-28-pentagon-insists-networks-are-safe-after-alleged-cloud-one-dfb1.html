Pentagon Insists Networks Are Safe After Alleged Cloud One Hack

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:40 PM PKT

Media reports revealed that hackers breached the US Air Force's Cloud One computing platform late Friday, creating concerns that sensitive military data might have been exposed, France 24 reported. Euronews noted that the US military insisted the Cloud One platform was secured following the news.

Without specifically denying that an intrusion occurred, the Pentagon emphasized that its broader computing networks remain secure amid an ongoing series of cyber-related data incidents, according to reporting by France 24 and Euronews.
