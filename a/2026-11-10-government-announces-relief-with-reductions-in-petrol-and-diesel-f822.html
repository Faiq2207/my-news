Government Announces Relief with Reductions in Petrol and Diesel Prices

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

According to 92 News, Dawn, and Aaj News, the government has slashed the prices of petrol and high-speed diesel. Dawn reported an 8 rupees per litre reduction in petrol and a 7 rupees drop for high-speed diesel, while Samaa noted that petrol fell to Rs338 per litre and diesel to Rs371.

The adjustment takes effect on November 11. Media reports noted this marks the sixth straight time that authorities have lowered fuel prices for the public.
