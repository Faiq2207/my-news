Pakistan and Malaysia Reaffirm Commitment to Defense Cooperation

Latest News Updates news desk, 24 November 2026, 7:10 AM PKT

CDF Asim Munir held talks with the Prime Minister of Malaysia to enhance defense ties between the two countries, according to 92 News. GNN reported that Malaysia reaffirmed its commitment to boosting bilateral defense collaboration.

During the meeting, both sides also underlined their shared dedication to maintaining regional peace, stability, and security, Dunya News reported.
