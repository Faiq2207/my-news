NAB Issues Second Call-Up Notice to Asad Mahmood Over Alleged Illegal Appointments

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

The National Accountability Bureau (NAB) issued a second call-up notice to Asad Mahmood, son of the JUI-F chief, over alleged illegal appointments made during his tenure, according to Aaj News and the Express Tribune. Mahmood has been directed to appear in person at 11am on November 13.

This follows an earlier notice issued by NAB regarding the same inquiry into alleged illegal appointments within Pakistan Post.
