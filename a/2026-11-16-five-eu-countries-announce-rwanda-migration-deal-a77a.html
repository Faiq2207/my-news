Five EU Countries Announce Rwanda Migration Deal

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:44 AM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, five European nations—including Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece, and the Netherlands—unveiled a partnership to establish a migrant deportation hub in Rwanda.

Al Jazeera and Euronews reported that the plan aims to handle rejected asylum seekers by 2027, despite facing joint opposition and warnings from numerous aid and rights groups.
