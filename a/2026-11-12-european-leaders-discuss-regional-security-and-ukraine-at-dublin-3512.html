European Leaders Discuss Regional Security and Ukraine at Dublin Summit

Latest News Updates news desk, 13 November 2026, 2:50 AM PKT

Leaders from more than 50 European countries and institutions gathered in Dublin for the European Political Community summit to discuss regional security collaboration and an envoy for Ukraine peace talks, according to Al Jazeera and France 24.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky warned European leaders to be wary of discussions between the United States and Russia regarding a new security architecture for the continent, France 24 reported.
