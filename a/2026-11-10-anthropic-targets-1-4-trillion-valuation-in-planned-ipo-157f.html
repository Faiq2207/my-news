Anthropic Targets $1.4 Trillion Valuation in Planned IPO

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

France 24 and Al Jazeera reported that US firm Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI chatbot, has announced plans for a blockbuster initial public offering.

According to France 24, the company will offer 825 million shares at a price between $70 and $78 apiece, giving the firm a valuation of around 1.4 trillion dollars. Al Jazeera added that the company is looking to raise up to $64.4bn in the debut.
