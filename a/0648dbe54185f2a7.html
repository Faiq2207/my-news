Prime Minister Shehbaz Arrives in Turkiye for COP31 Summit

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 2:04 AM PKT

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif landed in Antalya, Turkiye, on Tuesday to begin a three-day official visit, Dawn and Samaa reported. He was received upon arrival by Turkish officials.

The prime minister's visit aims at attending the Leaders' Summit of the 31st Conference of the Parties (COP 31) to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, according to Dawn. Dawn also noted that the prime minister is scheduled to deliver an address to the COP31 gathering.
