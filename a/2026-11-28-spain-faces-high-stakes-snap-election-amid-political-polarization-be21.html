Spain Faces High-Stakes Snap Election Amid Political Polarization

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:40 PM PKT

Spanish voters are heading to the polls on Sunday in a pivotal snap election that could end Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez's 8 years of leftist governance, DW and France 24 reported. A political shift to the right in Madrid could significantly alter Spain's policies regarding defense, migration, and the war in Gaza, according to DW.

Al Jazeera reported that opinion polls suggest the conservative PP and the far-right Vox could secure a governing majority. Sanchez issued a final plea urging citizens to vote in large numbers to prevent the nation from going backwards, DW noted.

France 24 highlighted that Vox centered its political campaign on immigration, a topic that intensified after tens of thousands of migrants crossed into Ceuta from Morocco in July.
