Russian Cargo Ship on Fire off Finland Following Drone Strike

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 4:15 PM PKT

According to Al Jazeera, a Russian cargo ship traveling toward Russia's Kaliningrad was damaged in international waters following a drone attack before drifting into Finnish territory, where it remains on fire.

France 24 reported that Finland warned the ongoing conflict was spilling over following the Baltic Sea drone strike on the Russian cargo vessel, emphasizing that Kyiv holds the right to defend itself against Moscow's aggression.
