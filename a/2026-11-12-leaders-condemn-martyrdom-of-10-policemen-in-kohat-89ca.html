Leaders Condemn Martyrdom of 10 Policemen in Kohat

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 3:59 PM PKT

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif expressed their sorrow following a terrorist attack on a police team in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s Kohat district. According to Dawn, the incident resulted in the martyrdom of 10 police personnel, while 18 terrorists were reported killed.

Following the tragedy, Aaj News noted that Prime Minister Shehbaz vowed there would be no compromise on terrorism as authorities continue their ongoing fight against militant groups.
