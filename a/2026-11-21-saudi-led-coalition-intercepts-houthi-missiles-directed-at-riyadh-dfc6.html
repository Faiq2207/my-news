Saudi-Led Coalition Intercepts Houthi Missiles Directed at Riyadh

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:56 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Al Jazeera, Iran-backed Houthi rebels in Yemen launched strikes toward Saudi Arabia, with the coalition reporting it intercepted and destroyed three ballistic missiles fired toward Riyadh.

Al Jazeera and NHK World noted that the incident marked consecutive days of emergency alerts and attacks targeting the Saudi capital, amid an ongoing exchange of strikes involving Houthi threats against regional ports and power stations.
