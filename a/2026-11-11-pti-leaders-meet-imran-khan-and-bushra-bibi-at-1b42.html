PTI Leaders Meet Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 2:55 AM PKT

According to Dawn, lawyers representing the PTI met with party founder Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi for three hours at Adiala jail on Wednesday. The meeting took place following Islamabad High Court orders issued while hearing appeals against their convictions.

92 News and ARY News also reported that Salman Safdar and Latif Khosa met the PTI founder and Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail after the court orders, with further details expected to be shared.
