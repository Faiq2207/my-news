ECO Meeting in Islamabad Focuses on Regional Cooperation and Energy

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 4:08 PM PKT

According to 92 News, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and other delegates arrived in Islamabad to attend the Economic Cooperation Organisation meeting. Dawn reported that Araghchi called for a reliable and sustainable regional energy mechanism during the 30th meeting of the ECO Council of Ministers.

Additionally, 92 News reported that Pakistani officials, including Ishaq Dar, urged Afghanistan to address terrorism for stronger regional cooperation, noting that action against terrorism is a requirement for economic cooperation.
