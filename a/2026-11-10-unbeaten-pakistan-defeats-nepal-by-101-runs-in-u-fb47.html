Unbeaten Pakistan Defeats Nepal by 101 Runs in U-19 Asia Cup

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

Defending champions Pakistan maintained their unbeaten run in the ACC Men’s U-19 Asia Cup 2026 by registering a convincing 101-run victory over Nepal at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground in Muscat, Dawn, ARY News, and Samaa reported. All-round performances, notably from Hussain, helped drive the team to success.

With three consecutive wins from three matches, Pakistan finished at the top of Group A. The victory successfully secured their qualification for the tournament's semi-finals against Bangladesh.
