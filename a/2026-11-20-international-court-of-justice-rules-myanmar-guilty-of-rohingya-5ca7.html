International Court of Justice rules Myanmar guilty of Rohingya genocide

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Al Jazeera, the International Court of Justice delivered a historic ruling finding Myanmar guilty of committing genocide against the Rohingya population in 2017.

Al Jazeera and France 24 noted that while refugees and international supporters viewed the verdict as a vital vindication, safe returns, citizenship rights, and reparations for the persecuted Muslim minority remain far from guaranteed.
