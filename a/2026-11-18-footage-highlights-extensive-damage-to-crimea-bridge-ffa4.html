Footage Highlights Extensive Damage to Crimea Bridge

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to DW and Euronews, a recent Ukrainian attack on the Kerch Bridge has heavily disrupted a vital transport link connecting mainland Russia to the annexed Crimean Peninsula. The strike is expected to severely impact Russian military logistics.

BBC News and Euronews reported that newly emerged video footage and analysis from teams like BBC Verify show the clear scale of destruction, including collapsed sections of the bridge resulting from the overnight strike.
