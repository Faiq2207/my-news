India Edge Pakistan by One Wicket in Last-Ball U19 Asia Cup Final

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 12:55 AM PKT

According to Dawn, India secured a dramatic one-wicket win over Pakistan on the final ball of the ACC Men's U-19 Asia Cup final at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground in Muscat. Pakistan set a target of 338 runs after batting first, aided by a century from Usman Khan as noted by ARY News and Geo News.

Dawn reported that India was reeling at 252 for eight before Mohit Ulva and Chigurupati Venkata held their nerve to finish the chase. The thrilling last-over finish was also reported by 92 News, Aaj News, and ARY News.
