Pakistan Lowers Petrol and Diesel Prices

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 2:04 AM PKT

The federal government has reduced petroleum prices, marking another downward revision. Dawn and The Express Tribune reported that the price of petrol has been slashed by Rs8 per litre, while high-speed diesel has decreased by Rs7 per litre. Aaj News noted that this represents the sixth straight time the government has reduced petrol and diesel rates.

Following the revision, petrol is now priced at Rs338 per litre and diesel costs Rs371 per litre, according to Samaa and The Express Tribune. The new rates take effect under a daily price mechanism for Nov 11, The Express Tribune reported, while ARY News confirmed that the cuts came as the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority issued a new notification.
