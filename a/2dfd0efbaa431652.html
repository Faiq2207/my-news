France Blames Iranian Cyber Unit for Supermarket Fridge Attacks

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 1:38 AM PKT

The French government has formally accused Iran's Revolutionary Guards of carrying out cyberattacks against supermarket refrigerators and freezers last month, according to BBC News, France 24, and DW. Foreign Minister Jean-Noel Barrot described the hacking operation as unprecedented, DW and Euronews reported.

France 24 reported that the Paris prosecutor launched an investigation into Shahid Kaveh, identified as a cyber unit of the IRGC. The attacks occurred on October 11 and 12, affecting more than 1,000 stores and spoiling tons of chilled and frozen food, according to Al Jazeera, BBC News, and France 24. Euronews added that France plans to summon the Iranian ambassador over the incident.
