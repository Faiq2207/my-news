US Flights Resume After Nationwide Ground Stop Caused by Data Overload

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 3:18 PM PKT

According to France 24 and the BBC, the Federal Aviation Administration issued a nationwide ground stop on Friday after routers became overloaded with data. More than 8,200 flights, representing over 31% of scheduled departures in the United States, were canceled during the disruption, which lasted nearly five hours and marked the longest such stoppage since September 11, 2001.

Transport Secretary Sean Duffy blamed the incident on an incredibly old and outdated air traffic control system, noting that systems were overloaded and that normal operations would take time to resume. Officials emphasized that the stoppage was not the result of a cyberattack or a Microsoft outage, as flights slowly began to get back on track.
