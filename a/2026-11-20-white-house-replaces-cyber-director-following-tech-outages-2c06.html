White House replaces cyber director following tech outages

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to CNBC, Al Jazeera, and DW, President Donald Trump has replaced National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross following a wave of widespread technology outages and a chaotic week of technical failures.

The newly appointed official, Kirsten Davies, previously served as the Pentagon's chief information officer. Al Jazeera noted that Davies stated the United States has an opportunity to dominate the cyber landscape for generations to come.
