Security Forces Eliminate 12 Terrorists in Balochistan Operations

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 5:23 PM PKT

According to Dawn and Dunya News, security forces killed 12 India-sponsored terrorists in four separate intelligence-based operations (IBOs) conducted in Balochistan between November 20 and 23.

The military’s media wing, the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), stated that the operations targeted a terrorist hideout in the province.
