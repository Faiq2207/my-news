Pakistan Reach 281-5 on Opening Day of Sri Lanka Test

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 1:38 AM PKT

According to Dawn, Pakistan concluded day one of the first Test match against Sri Lanka with a total of 281-5 on Monday. Four batters managed to record half-centuries during the innings.

The team's captain, Babar Azam, reached 50 runs, Dawn reported. In addition, two players making their Test debuts contributed significantly, with opener Tayyab Arif scoring 57 and Mohammad Taha registering 51.
