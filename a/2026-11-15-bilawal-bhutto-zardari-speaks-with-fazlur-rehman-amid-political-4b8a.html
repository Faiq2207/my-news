Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari Speaks With Fazlur Rehman Amid Political Tensions

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari contacted Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman by telephone on Sunday, according to reports by Dawn, ARY News, and Aaj News. The leaders discussed the overall political situation.

Dawn and Aaj News reported that Bilawal condemned recent government actions and alleged political vengeance targeted at the JUI-F chief amid existing tensions with the PML-N-led federal administration. Samaa added that the PPP chairman also urged the government to tolerate criticism.
