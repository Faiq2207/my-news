Thousands Rally in Istanbul Against Media Crackdown

Latest News Updates news desk, 23 November 2026, 5:27 PM PKT

According to France 24, several thousand people marched through Istanbul on Sunday to protest a growing crackdown on independent and opposition media outlets. The demonstrations followed police raids on pro-opposition broadcaster Sozcu TV, during which authorities installed a government-appointed trustee and arrested senior staff members.

France 24 reported that the protest was organized by the main opposition Yeni Parti. The marches highlight rising public anger over mounting restrictions on press freedom and political opposition in Turkey.
