Portuguese Foreign Minister Paulo Rangel Arrives in Pakistan for Official Visit

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 4:15 PM PKT

According to Dawn and Dunya News, Minister of State and Foreign Affairs of Portugal Paulo Rangel arrived in Pakistan on Sunday for an official visit. The Foreign Office stated that Rangel is visiting the country from November 22 to 23.

Dawn reported that Rangel is visiting at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar and was duly received upon his arrival.
