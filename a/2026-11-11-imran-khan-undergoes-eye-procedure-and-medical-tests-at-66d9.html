Imran Khan Undergoes Eye Procedure and Medical Tests at PIMS

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

Former prime minister and PTI founder Imran Khan was transported from Adiala Jail in Rawalpindi to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) in Islamabad for follow-up medical care and an eye procedure, according to Dawn and ARY News.

Following the examination, PIMS declared his physical condition stable and satisfactory, with normal vital signs, as reported by ARY News, Samaa, and Aaj News. He was subsequently shifted back to Adiala Jail, according to Dunya News and ARY News.
