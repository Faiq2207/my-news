Pakistan Reaffirms Commitment to Saudi Arabia's Security

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 3:59 PM PKT

The Director-General of the Inter-Services Public Relations (DG ISPR) declared that the protection of Saudi Arabia and the Holy Mosques remains Pakistan's top priority, emphasizing that the nation's commitment to Saudi security is non-negotiable.

According to the Express Tribune, this statement accompanied remarks regarding a meeting between representatives of the Makkah pact and Iran aimed at establishing a framework for de-escalation, with Pakistan prepared to take necessary defensive measures.
