FBR Eliminates Surcharge for New Income Tax Filers

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

The Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) has eliminated a Rs25,000 surcharge previously charged to individuals joining the tax system, according to ARY News and Dunya News.

The waiver applies to new taxpayers aiming to be included on the active taxpayer list, Dunya News reported.
