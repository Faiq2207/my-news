Submarine Cable Fault Likely to Cause Internet Disruptions Across Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) stated that users across Pakistan may experience internet connectivity and service disruptions due to a fault in an international submarine cable system, according to Dawn and Samaa.

The PTA reported that the issue originated from the SEA-ME-WE 5 (SMW5) cable system, which encountered a fault on Wednesday.
