Pakistan Stock Exchange Surges Past 180,000 Mark

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

The Pakistan Stock Exchange sustained a bullish run, with Dunya News initially noting a rise of more than 300 points.

Express Tribune reported that the benchmark KSE-100 index surged by 1,111 points to cross 180,000. Market activity recorded 214.29 million shares traded, amounting to a total turnover value of Rs20.27 billion.
