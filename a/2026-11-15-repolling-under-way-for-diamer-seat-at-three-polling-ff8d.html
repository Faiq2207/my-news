Repolling Under Way for Diamer Seat at Three Polling Stations

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

Repolling is under way at three polling stations for the GBA-16 (Diamer-II) seat in Gilgit, with a contest between PPP's Attaullah Shah and Istehkam-i-Pakistan Party's Imam Malik, Dawn reported.

Dawn stated that a total of 2,780 registered voters are eligible to cast their votes in the ongoing polling, which is scheduled to continue until 5pm.
