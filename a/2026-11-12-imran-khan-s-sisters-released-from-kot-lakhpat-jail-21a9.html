Imran Khan's Sisters Released from Kot Lakhpat Jail

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 3:59 PM PKT

The sisters of incarcerated PTI founder Imran Khan, Dr Uzma Khan and Noreen Niazi, have been released from Kot Lakhpat jail. According to Dawn, their release followed the withdrawal of their extended detention orders, as confirmed by PTI legal counsel Rana Mudassar Umer.

The legal counsel announced the development through a post on X, noting that the two individuals had previously been detained before the reversal of their extended confinement orders.
