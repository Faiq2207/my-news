Severe Protests and Closures Trigger Traffic Jams in Karachi

Latest News Updates news desk, 27 November 2026, 4:48 AM PKT

Commuters across Karachi experienced extensive travel difficulties and gridlock on Thursday. Karachi Traffic Police stated that the Superhighway near Al Asif Square was shut down, with both inbound and outbound lanes closed to vehicular movement due to an ongoing protest, according to Dawn.

ARY News and Aaj News reported widespread gridlock across major roads and motorways. ARY News added that police released alternative routes for motorists following the closure of the highway.
