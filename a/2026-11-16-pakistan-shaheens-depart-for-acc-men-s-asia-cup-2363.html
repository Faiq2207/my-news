Pakistan Shaheens Depart for ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 4:08 PM PKT

According to Aaj News and ARY News, the Pakistan Shaheens departed to feature in the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament, with Aaj News noting their travel to Doha to defend their title.

Express Tribune reported that Abdul Samad leads the 15-member squad participating in the regional competition.
