US and Iran Exchange Conflicting Signals on War and Negotiations

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 3:33 PM PKT

United States President Donald Trump stated that the war with Iran is effectively over and described additional strikes as quite unlikely, according to Euronews and BBC News. However, Iranian officials rejected assertions that diplomatic discussions are underway.
