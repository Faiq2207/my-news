Russian Strikes Hit Kyiv, Damaging Embassies and Historic Cathedral

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 3:39 AM PKT

A wave of daytime Russian strikes on Kyiv killed one person and damaged a historic cathedral alongside the embassies of Austria and the Netherlands, France 24 reported. Loud explosions were heard throughout the Ukrainian capital during the attacks.

The strikes occurred while the leaders of Russia, China, and the United States held a separate summit, according to the same report from France 24.
