Donald Trump Challenges US Midterm Election Outcomes

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 1:38 AM PKT

United States President Donald Trump brought forward allegations of voting fraud on Monday to challenge the integrity of the midterm elections, Dawn reported. His remarks followed an outcome in which the Democratic Party captured both chambers of Congress, delivering a major loss to his Republican Party, according to Dawn.

The Express Tribune reported that Trump questioned whether the November 3 midterm elections were honest. He pointed to large ballot dumps during early voting and asserted that their numbers made overcoming the deficit impossible, according to the Express Tribune. Geo News also noted that Trump returned to election fraud allegations after an initial concession.
