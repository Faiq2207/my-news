England Stuns New Zealand With Record Rugby Victory and Leeds Beats Chelsea

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 1:53 AM PKT

According to BBC News, England stunned New Zealand by beating the three-time world champions by 25 points, marking a record margin of victory. Former captain Lewis Moody joined the current team to face the haka in an emotional start to the match at Allianz Stadium.

In football news reported by BBC News and Al Jazeera, Leeds United secured their first win at Chelsea since 1999 with a 4-1 victory at Stamford Bridge. Noah Okafor scored twice for Leeds, and manager Daniel Farke expressed delight at creating a piece of history, while Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso faced mounting pressure after the defeat.
