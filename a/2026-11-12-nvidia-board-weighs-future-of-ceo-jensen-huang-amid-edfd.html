Nvidia Board Weighs Future of CEO Jensen Huang Amid Affair Reports

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 3:59 PM PKT

Nvidia's board of directors held meetings to decide whether to remove chief executive Jensen Huang, according to reports by France 24 and CNBC. The leadership crisis follows revelations that the company co-founder had two extramarital affairs. One relationship reportedly involved Alice Huang, a senior executive at the Singapore-based firm Megaspeed, Euronews reported, while another involved an individual alleged to be a Chinese intelligence operative.

The controversy has sparked geopolitical friction, with China accusing the United States of creating spy stories to justify a technology blockade, according to France 24. Meanwhile, the turmoil sent Nvidia shares tumbling by more than 8% in after-hours trading on Wednesday, Euronews noted, while CNBC reported a 6% decline as investors tracked developments surrounding the artificial intelligence chip manufacturer.
