Nvidia CEO Faces Scrutiny Over Alleged Espionage Ties

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 3:33 PM PKT

United States President Donald Trump stated that the FBI is examining allegations surrounding Nvidia Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang, according to reports by Samaa, Aaj News, and ARY News. Dawn reported that Trump commented on Monday that Huang was suffering greatly and was unaware that a woman he had an affair with was an alleged Chinese spy.

While US authorities review the matter, ARY News reported that China has formally denied the espionage claims connected to the Nvidia controversy.
