Five EU Nations Negotiating Migrant Return Hub in Rwanda

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 4:08 PM PKT

Denmark, Austria, Germany, the Netherlands, and Greece are in the final stages of negotiations with the government in Kigali to set up a migrant return hub in Rwanda, according to France 24 and Euronews.

The proposed facility would become the first migration deportation centre operated by EU member states outside of the bloc. The pilot project could be announced on Monday and potentially become operational by mid-2027, following the EU's toughening of migration rules.
