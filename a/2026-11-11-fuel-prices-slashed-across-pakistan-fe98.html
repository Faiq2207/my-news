Fuel Prices Slashed Across Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 2:55 AM PKT

According to Dawn and ARY News, fuel prices across Pakistan have been slashed once again. High-speed diesel was cut by Rs7 per litre, while petrol prices saw a reduction of Rs3 following a notification by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA).

Samaa reported that the petrol price fell to Rs335 per litre, while diesel dropped to Rs364. Aaj News also confirmed that the government has reduced petrol and diesel prices further.
