World Bank Approves Financing for Sindh Water and Sanitation

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

The World Bank has approved $300 million in financing aimed at expanding access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene services for 1.5 million people in selected rural areas of Sindh, Dawn reported.

According to Dawn, the financing will specifically support the Sindh Transformational Accelerated Rural Water Supply, Sanitation, and Hygiene project.
