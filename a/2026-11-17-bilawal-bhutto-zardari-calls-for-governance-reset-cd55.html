Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari Calls for Governance Reset

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:46 PM PKT

Speaking at the Karachi Press Club, PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari stated that the country's governance system had not simply collapsed, but was rather "made to collapse," according to Dawn. Express Tribune and Geo News also reported his remarks calling for a political reset.

According to Express Tribune, Bilawal asserted that Pakistan cannot function without the PTI, describing it as a political reality regardless of personal preferences.
