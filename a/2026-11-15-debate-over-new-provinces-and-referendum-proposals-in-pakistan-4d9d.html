Debate Over New Provinces and Referendum Proposals in Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

Federal Health Minister and MQM-P leader Syed Mustafa Kamal described the creation of smaller provinces as a national necessity rather than a political desire, arguing that the existing four-province structure has failed, according to Dawn. ARY News reported that Kamal also emphasized that December is vital for a referendum on new provinces.

Dunya News noted remarks by Salman Akram Raja stating that direct talks with Imran Khan are needed regarding the new provinces issue. Meanwhile, Dawn reported that President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif agreed that any move to create new provinces must follow the Constitution and receive parliament's nod, with Zardari reiterating opposition to dividing Sindh.
