Fuel Prices Reduced Across Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:44 AM PKT

According to 92 News, the government has reduced diesel and petrol prices effective for November 17. ARY News, Samaa, and the Express Tribune similarly reported that the prices of petrol and high-speed diesel were cut by Rs2 per litre, with the Express Tribune specifying that the new price for petrol is set at Rs328 while high-speed diesel stands at Rs358.
