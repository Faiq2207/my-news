Nvidia CEO Faces Scrutiny Over Alleged Chinese Spy Ties

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 3:33 PM PKT

United States President Donald Trump revealed that the FBI is examining Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang over an affair involving an alleged Chinese government agent, according to reporting from Euronews, Sky News, and BBC News. Trump explained that he discussed the matter directly with Huang, who maintained that he did not know the individual was a spy, CNBC and Euronews reported.

The revelations have stirred wider political and financial repercussions. Al Jazeera reported that American lawmakers are pushing for stricter limits on semiconductor exports to China as inquiries proceed. Meanwhile, CNBC reported that Nvidia's stock edged upward as market participants assess the fallout, with Trump continuing to affirm his confidence in the technology executive.
