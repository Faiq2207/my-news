Second Pakistan-Sri Lanka Test Relocated to Rawalpindi Due to Smog

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) announced that the second Test match between Pakistan and Sri Lanka has been moved from Lahore to Rawalpindi, according to 92 News, Dawn, and the Express Tribune. The relocation was decided due to worsening smog conditions prevailing in the Punjab capital.

The fixture, originally set to be played in Lahore from November 17 to 21, will now take place at the Rawalpindi venue as confirmed by official PCB announcements and media reports.
