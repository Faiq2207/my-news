Rio de Janeiro Hosts 31st Annual Pride Parade on Copacabana Beach

Latest News Updates news desk, 23 November 2026, 5:27 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, thousands of people flocked to Copacabana Beach in Rio de Janeiro on Sunday, 22 November 2026, to take part in the 31st annual Pride Parade. Colorful costumes and dancing filled the beachfront as attendees celebrated LGBT+ diversity and called for greater equality.

France 24 noted that the milestone event marked 15 years since Brazil's Supreme Court unanimously recognized same-sex unions, serving as an important cultural and political platform for the community.
