Short-Term Inflation Reaches 10.41pc Driven by Wheat Costs

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:15 AM PKT

According to Dunya News and Dawn, Pakistan's weekly inflation fell 0.65 percent over the short term, but the annual rate settled at 10.41 percent for the week ending Nov 19.

The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics stated that the index increase was driven largely by rising wheat flour prices across measured items.
