Ishaq Dar Closes ECO Meeting and Announces Makkah Alliance Summit

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:44 AM PKT

According to Dawn and Samaa, Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar spoke at the 30th meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Council of Ministers in Islamabad, calling for nations to translate collective commitments into concrete action and stressing the need for joint action to address shared challenges.

In addition, Dawn reported that Dar announced Pakistan will host the next meeting of the Makkah Defence Alliance's apex committee later this month.
