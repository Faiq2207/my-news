Top UN Court Rules Myanmar Committed Rohingya Genocide

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 3:39 AM PKT

The International Court of Justice has delivered an unprecedented verdict finding that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya minority during a military crackdown in 2017 that killed thousands, according to BBC News and Al Jazeera. Rohingya refugees in Bangladesh and activists cheered the judgment.

Al Jazeera reported that this landmark decision in The Hague marks the first time an entire state has been held responsible for genocide at the international court.
