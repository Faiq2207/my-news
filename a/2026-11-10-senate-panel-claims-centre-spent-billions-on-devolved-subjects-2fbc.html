Senate Panel Claims Centre Spent Billions on Devolved Subjects in Constitutional Violation

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

The head of a Senate panel stated on Tuesday that the federal government spent more than Rs5 trillion on devolved provincial subjects and ministries created on Council of Common Interests matters in violation of the Constitution, according to Dawn and the Express Tribune. The panel cited data sourced from a report by the Auditor General.

In response to these findings, the Senate body barred further releases of funds to 17 ministries and issued a warning to the finance ministry regarding the expenditure.
