Turnout Drops During Sixth Consecutive Saturday of Protests in France

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 1:53 AM PKT

According to France 24, turnout was down at largely peaceful demonstrations across France on the sixth consecutive Saturday of protests over the cost of living, with the interior ministry putting the figure at 82,500 across the country, while organisers said 150,000 people participated. Euronews reported that thousands of people demonstrated in Portugal and France on Saturday, calling for higher wages, affordable housing, and action to tackle the rising cost of living.

France 24 also noted that the interior ministry recorded 44 demonstrations nationwide and seven arrests by 2pm local time, describing the situation as generally calm.
