English Village Votes Symbolically to Leave the UK Over Asylum Plans

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 4:15 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, residents of the small English village of Barnham participated in a symbolic referendum to 'leave the UK' in protest against government proposals.

The reports stated that the local backlash was triggered by plans to accommodate approximately 930 asylum seekers at a nearby former air force base, a figure exceeding the village's own population of 584 people.
