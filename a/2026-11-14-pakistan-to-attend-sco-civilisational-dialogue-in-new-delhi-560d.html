Pakistan to Attend SCO Civilisational Dialogue in New Delhi

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 12:55 AM PKT

According to the Foreign Office, as reported by Dawn, Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik has been nominated to represent Pakistan at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Civilisational Dialogue. Dawn stated that the gathering is scheduled to take place in New Delhi from Nov 20-22.

ARY News, Samaa, and Aaj News also reported on Pakistan's planned participation in the New Delhi event, with Dawn noting that representatives from various member states will attend.
