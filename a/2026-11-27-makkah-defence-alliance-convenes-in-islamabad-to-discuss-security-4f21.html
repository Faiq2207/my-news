Makkah Defence Alliance Convenes in Islamabad to Discuss Security

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:25 AM PKT

According to Dawn, a meeting of the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement's top decision-making body, the Strategic Political and Defence Committee, was held in the federal capital to review regional security and condemn Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia. Aaj News, 92 News, and ARY News reported similar developments, noting that Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Türkiye agreed to boost defence cooperation and issued a joint communiqué following the Islamabad meetings.
