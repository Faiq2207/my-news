Sindh Government Forms Ministerial Body for Local Government Consultations

Latest News Updates news desk, 27 November 2026, 5:10 PM PKT

According to Dawn, Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah announced a comprehensive consultation process regarding a new local government law amidst ongoing discussions about new provinces. The provincial administration aims to empower local governance through structured dialogue.

As part of this initiative, the CM has formed a two-member ministerial committee tasked with holding official talks with all relevant opposition stakeholders. Furthermore, the government confirmed that the upcoming local government elections will be held on schedule.
