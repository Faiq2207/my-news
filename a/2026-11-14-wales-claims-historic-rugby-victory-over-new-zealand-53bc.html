Wales Claims Historic Rugby Victory Over New Zealand

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 12:55 AM PKT

According to France 24 and the BBC, Wales claimed a historic 35-28 victory over New Zealand in Cardiff, securing their first win against the All Blacks since 1953. BBC News reported that Welsh captain Dewi Lake stated he always believed his team could defeat New Zealand and end their 73-year hoodoo.

In the same reports, New Zealand captain Beauden Barrett praised the brick wall defense put up by Wales after the All Blacks were stunned in Cardiff.
