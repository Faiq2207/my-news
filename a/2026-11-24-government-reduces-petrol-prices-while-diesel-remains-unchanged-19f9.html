Government Reduces Petrol Prices While Diesel Remains Unchanged

Latest News Updates news desk, 24 November 2026, 7:10 AM PKT

The Pakistani government has reduced the price of petrol by Rs3 per litre, while leaving the rate of diesel unchanged, according to Samaa and Dawn.

ARY News reported that the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority announced the reduction in the petrol price, while Aaj News also confirmed the decrease in petrol rates alongside stable diesel prices.
