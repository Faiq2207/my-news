Government Receives First 15,000-Tonne LPG Cargo from Abu Dhabi

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to Dawn, the government announced on Wednesday that it received an initial cargo of 15,000 tonnes of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) shipped from Abu Dhabi.

The import follows policy changes introduced earlier in the month allowing state-owned enterprises to arrange imports aimed at easing domestic supply shortages and managing rising costs.
