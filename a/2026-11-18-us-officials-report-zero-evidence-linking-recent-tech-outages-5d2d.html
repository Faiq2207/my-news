US Officials Report Zero Evidence Linking Recent Tech Outages

Latest News Updates news desk, 18 November 2026, 4:34 PM PKT

According to France 24 and BBC News, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio insisted that authorities have found zero evidence connecting recent widespread outages across Microsoft, banks, and air traffic operations.

Rubio stated there is no proof that the technical disruptions are related to one another or caused by nefarious actors, addressing concerns following multiple system failures.
