Islamabad High Court Extends Suspension of Federal Capital Local Government Polls

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 5:23 PM PKT

According to Dawn, Justice Umair Majeed Malik of the Islamabad High Court (IHC) heard petitions on Thursday and extended the interim order suspending local government elections in the federal capital by another two weeks.

The court also sought para-wise comments from the Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) regarding the matter.
