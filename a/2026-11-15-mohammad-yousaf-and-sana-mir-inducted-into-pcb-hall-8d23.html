Mohammad Yousaf and Sana Mir Inducted into PCB Hall of Fame

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

Former Pakistan cricket stars Mohammad Yousaf and Sana Mir have been inducted into the PCB Hall of Fame, according to Geo News and Dunya News. The recognition honors their significant contributions to the sport.

The Express Tribune also reported on the induction, noting that the former players are recognized for their remarkable contributions to the game of cricket in Pakistan.
