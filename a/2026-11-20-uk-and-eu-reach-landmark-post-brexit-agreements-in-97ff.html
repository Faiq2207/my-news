UK and EU Reach Landmark Post-Brexit Agreements in Brussels

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:15 AM PKT

The United Kingdom and the European Union reached a series of agreements on Friday during a summit held in Brussels, aiming to rebuild diplomatic and economic ties ten years after the Brexit referendum. According to France 24 and the BBC, the landmark deal establishes agreements regarding British food export checks, youth mobility and experience schemes, and carbon emissions trading quotas.

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham described the outcome as a turning point and a good day for Britain and Europe, France 24 reported. European Union chief Ursula von der Leyen similarly welcomed the reset in bilateral ties, while Deutsche Welle and Euronews noted that leaders viewed the summit as a major step forward despite lingering domestic debates over long-term post-Brexit relations.
