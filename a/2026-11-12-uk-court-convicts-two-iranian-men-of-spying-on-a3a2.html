UK Court Convicts Two Iranian Men of Spying on Journalists

Latest News Updates news desk, 13 November 2026, 2:50 AM PKT

A London court found two Iranian citizens guilty of surveilling reporters for a Persian-language television station critical of Iran's government, according to DW. Al Jazeera reported that the pair were promised rewards to send intelligence back to Iran.

According to DW, one of the men was also convicted of planning a violent attack against the journalists. Al Jazeera and Fox News noted that the convictions highlight Tehran's broader reach and shadow operations across Europe.
