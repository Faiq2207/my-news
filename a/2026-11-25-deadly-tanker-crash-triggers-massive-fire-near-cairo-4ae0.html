Deadly Tanker Crash Triggers Massive Fire Near Cairo

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 4:36 AM PKT

A catastrophic vehicle fire on a major ring road near Cairo has resulted in heavy casualties. BBC News reported that 31 people were killed and another 21 sustained injuries after a fuel tanker overturned on the highway. DW also noted that at least 31 individuals lost their lives in the crash on the Ring Road in Giza, adding that health authorities cautioned the death toll could increase as dozens of ambulances intervened.

Other outlets offered slightly different casualty numbers. Al Jazeera initially reported that at least 30 people were killed and 15 were injured when a vehicle transporting petroleum products flipped and caught fire, while also mentioning in another report that a truck carrying gas cylinders had burned. Euronews similarly stated that at least 30 people died after an overturned fuel truck ignited vehicles on the highway, with officials warning the fatality count might rise.
