European Allies Meet in Berlin to Show Support for Ukraine

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 4:33 PM PKT

France 24 reported that Ukraine's main European allies gathered in Berlin for a show of support alongside US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. The summit brought together leaders from Germany, France, Britain, Poland, and Italy, as well as NATO chief Mark Rutte.

Al Jazeera noted that the meeting addresses pressing financial concerns, as Kyiv faces a 25 billion-euro ($27.4bn) financing gap for the upcoming year.
