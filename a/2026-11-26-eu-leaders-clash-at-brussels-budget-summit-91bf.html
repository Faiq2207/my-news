EU Leaders Clash at Brussels Budget Summit

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 5:23 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, European Union leaders met in Brussels for a summit focused on the bloc's seven-year budget for 2028 to 2034. European Council President Antonio Costa urged leaders to seek compromise and listen to each other to secure a budget deal before the end of the year.

France 24 and Euronews also reported that the discussions involve deep divisions over a proposed €45 billion loan to keep Ukraine funded through 2027. Additionally, France is seeking to protect subsidies for the Common Agricultural Policy.
