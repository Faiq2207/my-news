Pope Leo XIV Draws Huge Crowds in Argentina

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

According to Euronews, Pope Leo XIV celebrated Mass before an estimated 650,000 people in Córdoba, Argentina. The pontiff greeted crowds from his popemobile and urged worshippers to open their hearts and embrace their faith.

Euronews and Al Jazeera reported that faithful from all over Argentina, ranging from the far north to Patagonia, drove hours to attend the service. Al Jazeera noted that the landmark visit drew crowds not seen for decades as the Pope urged Argentinians to emulate their saints.
