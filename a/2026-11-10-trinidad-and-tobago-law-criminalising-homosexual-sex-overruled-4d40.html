Trinidad and Tobago Law Criminalising Homosexual Sex Overruled

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

DW and BBC News reported that a London court, the Privy Council, has overruled a law in Trinidad and Tobago that criminalised consensual sexual relations between male same-sex couples.

BBC News and Sky News noted that because of its colonial legacy, the council serves as the independent country's last court of appeal. BBC News added that the ruling brings an end to an 11-year legal battle by dual-citizen Jason Jones.
