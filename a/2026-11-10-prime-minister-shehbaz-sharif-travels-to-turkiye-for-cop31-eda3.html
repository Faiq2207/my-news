Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif Travels to Turkiye for COP31 Summit

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 3:33 PM PKT

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif left for Turkiye on Tuesday to take part in the 31st Conference of the Parties (COP31) to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, according to reporting by Dawn, Aaj News, Geo News, and Dunya News.

Dawn and Dunya News reported that the premier's visit to Antalya covers the dates Nov 10-12. According to Dawn, Sharif is scheduled to address the Leaders' Summit tomorrow and is accompanied on the journey by PPP leader Sherry Rehman.
