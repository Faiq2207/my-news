Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi Plead for Medical Treatment in Letters and Petitions

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:44 AM PKT

According to Dawn, jailed former prime minister Imran Khan wrote an open letter to top judges, including the chief justices of the Federal Constitutional Court, Supreme Court, and Islamabad High Court, pleading for medical treatment for himself and his wife, Bushra Bibi, and stating he might not return alive.

ARY News reported that Bushra Bibi separately moved the Islamabad High Court (IHC) to request a hospital transfer, claiming that snakes were found in her prison cell.
