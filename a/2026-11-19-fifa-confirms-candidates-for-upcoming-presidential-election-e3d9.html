FIFA Confirms Candidates for Upcoming Presidential Election

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 4:33 PM PKT

BBC News, France 24, and Sky News reported that FIFA confirmed France's Philippe Diallo and Japan's Kozo Tashima will run against incumbent Gianni Infantino in the upcoming presidential election.

France 24 noted that Infantino faced prior criticism regarding proposed plans to sell stakes in World Cup profits to private investors, while former FIFA president Sepp Blatter claimed Infantino was ineligible due to running for a fourth time.
