Nvidia Posts Strong Third-Quarter Results Amid Leadership Challenges

Latest News Updates news desk, 18 November 2026, 4:34 PM PKT

According to France 24 and CNBC, AI chipmaker Nvidia posted third-quarter revenues that doubled last year's figures and rose 20 percent higher than the previous quarter.

France 24 and CNBC reported that the strong financial results coincide with a tumultuous period for the company, as the board maintains no intention of reinstating former CEO Jensen Huang while interim CEO Colette Kress addresses a divided workforce.
