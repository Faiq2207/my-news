Russia Bans Two French Lawmakers and Two Journalists in Tit-for-Tat Escalation

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 2:55 AM PKT

France 24 and Al Jazeera reported that Moscow announced bans preventing two French lawmakers and two journalists from entering Russia. According to the Kremlin, the measure was a tit-for-tat response to Paris expelling pro-Russian TV commentator Xenia Fedorova earlier in the year.

Euronews added that the EU has previously accused the former RT France chief of spreading Kremlin talking points on French television, while one of the targeted French figures viewed the ban as recognition of his work against foreign interference.
