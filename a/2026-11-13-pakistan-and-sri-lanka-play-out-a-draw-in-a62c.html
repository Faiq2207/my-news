Pakistan and Sri Lanka Play Out a Draw in First Test Affected by Bad Weather

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 2:25 AM PKT

According to Dawn and Express Tribune, Pakistan foiled Sri Lanka's bid for victory as the first Test ended in a draw on the fifth and final day in Rawalpindi. Led by Saud Shakeel and aided by bad weather, Pakistan held on after Sri Lanka sensed victory when reducing Pakistan to 151-5 at lunch with a lead of 139.

Samaa and ARY News similarly reported that Pakistan and Sri Lanka shared honours after the drawn Rawalpindi Test, while 92 News noted that Babar Azam's century drought continued during the match.
