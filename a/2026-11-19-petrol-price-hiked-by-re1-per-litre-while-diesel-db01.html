Petrol Price Hiked by Re1 per Litre While Diesel Remains Unchanged

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 3:39 AM PKT

According to Dunya News, Dawn, and ARY News, the government has snapped its petrol price reduction spree by raising the rate by Re1 per litre. OGRA confirmed that the diesel rate remains unchanged.

Samaa also reported the price adjustment, noting the increase in petrol alongside the steady pricing for diesel.
