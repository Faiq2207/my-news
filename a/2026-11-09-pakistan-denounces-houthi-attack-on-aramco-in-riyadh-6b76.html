Pakistan Denounces Houthi Attack on Aramco in Riyadh

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 1:38 AM PKT

Pakistan has strongly condemned an attack by Houthis on the corporate office of Saudi oil firm Aramco in Riyadh, according to reports from Dawn, Aaj News, and ARY News. Dawn reported that the incident occurred on Sunday when an Aramco facility was targeted.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs demanded that the Houthi group stop all strikes directed at Saudi Arabia unconditionally and immediately, Dawn reported. ARY News also recorded Pakistan's formal condemnation of the attempted attack on the facility.
