Pakistan Shaheens Defeat Nepal in Asia Cup Rising Stars

Latest News Updates news desk, 25 November 2026, 4:53 AM PKT

An unbeaten half-century by Rohail Nazir, followed by a devastating bowling display, helped Pakistan Shaheens defeat Nepal by 96 runs in their final Group B match of the Asia Cup Rising Stars at the University of Doha for Science and Technology, according to Dawn.

Dawn reported that the defending champions remained unbeaten, with Geo News and Aaj News also reporting that the Pakistan Shaheens crushed Nepal to reach the semi-finals.
