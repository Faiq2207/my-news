Jensen Huang Reinstated as Nvidia Chief Executive

Latest News Updates news desk, 24 November 2026, 7:10 AM PKT

France 24 and Al Jazeera reported that Nvidia's board has reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive and president in a stunning reversal. Huang had previously resigned amid an investigation regarding an extra-marital affair.

According to CNBC and France 24, US President Donald Trump stated that he personally contacted the company's board and provided assurances that Huang would not pose a national security risk. Meanwhile, lawmakers and Wall Street figures have expressed mixed reactions and vowed probes into the intervention, as noted by France 24 and CNBC.
