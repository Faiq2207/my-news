Tatjana Smith Switches Allegiance to the Netherlands

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to France 24, South Africa's sports minister ordered an official inquiry into the national swimming federation following the decision of the country's most decorated Olympic athlete, Tatjana Smith, to switch her competitive allegiance to the Netherlands.

The two-time Olympic champion announced her departure in an emotional social media video, prompting renewed domestic scrutiny over athlete support structures within South African sports, as reported by France 24.
