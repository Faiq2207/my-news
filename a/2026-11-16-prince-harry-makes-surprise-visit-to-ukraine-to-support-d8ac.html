Prince Harry Makes Surprise Visit to Ukraine to Support Wounded Veterans

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 4:08 PM PKT

British royal Prince Harry made a surprise weekend visit to Ukraine, where he attended a conference for medics organised by Superhumans, a rehabilitation clinic for wounded soldiers in the city, according to Euronews and BBC News.

During his appearance, he took to the stage and praised the resilience of the nation, telling war veterans and attendees that they were going to win the war.
