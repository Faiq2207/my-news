Pakistan Tightens Grip on Second Test Against Sri Lanka

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to Dawn, Pakistan strengthened its position on Wednesday during the second day of the second Test at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium, building a 227-run first-innings lead.

The hosts subsequently reduced Sri Lanka to 74-3 by stumps, following a half-century by wicket-keeper-batter Ghazi Ghori and contributions from other teammates.
