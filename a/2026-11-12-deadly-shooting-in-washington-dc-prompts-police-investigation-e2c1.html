Deadly Shooting in Washington DC Prompts Police Investigation

Latest News Updates news desk, 13 November 2026, 2:50 AM PKT

Police in the US capital investigated a shooting around a mile from the White House that led to one person being killed, Al Jazeera reported. France 24 noted that at least five people were shot in a DC neighborhood near the White House.

According to Al Jazeera and France 24, a swarm of officers responded to a nearby apartment building where a suspect was barricaded and later shot himself.
