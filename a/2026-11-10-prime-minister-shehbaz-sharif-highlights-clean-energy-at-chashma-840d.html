Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif Highlights Clean Energy at Chashma-5 Nuclear Plant Ceremony

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif performed the dome placement ceremony at the Chashma-5 nuclear power plant near Mianwali, ARY News and Dawn reported. Speaking at the event, the premier stated that the C-5 plant represents clean electricity and energy security for the people of Pakistan.

Broadcasted remarks highlighted the administration's vision for an energy-independent nation. The project milestone was celebrated as a significant step forward in fulfilling clean energy needs.
