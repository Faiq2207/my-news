Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar emphasizes independent foreign policy and multilateralism

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:56 PM PKT

According to Dawn and Express Tribune, Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar stressed the importance of multilateralism during an interview with Euronews. He stated that unilateralism and bloc politics would not work in addressing global challenges.

The foreign minister also affirmed that Pakistan's foreign policy remains very clear and independent, as reported by Dawn.
