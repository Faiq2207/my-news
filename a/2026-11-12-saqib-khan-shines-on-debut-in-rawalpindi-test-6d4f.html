Saqib Khan Shines on Debut in Rawalpindi Test

Latest News Updates news desk, 13 November 2026, 2:50 AM PKT

According to Dawn and Samaa, fast bowler Saqib Khan took 7 wickets for 130 runs on his debut during the first Test against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi, sparking a batting collapse for the visiting team on an overcast morning.

Saqib became the fourth Pakistani bowler to take seven wickets on debut, though rain and bad light subsequently interrupted the match.
