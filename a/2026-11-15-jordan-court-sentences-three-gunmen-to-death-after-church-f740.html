Jordan Court Sentences Three Gunmen to Death After Church Attack

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

According to France 24, a Jordanian court sentenced the three gunmen responsible for a terror attack on a church funeral procession in the village of Hamoud that killed nine people and wounded others. Al Jazeera reported that the three men were sentenced to death by hanging after being convicted of carrying out the attack in the Karak governorate.

France 24 also noted that the deadly incident prompted retaliatory strikes on terrorist organizations in neighboring Syria.
