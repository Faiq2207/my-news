Thousands Join Coordinated Protests Across Europe Over High Living Costs

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:15 AM PKT

A coordinated protest movement saw thousands of people take to the streets across multiple European nations on Friday, Euronews reported. The demonstrations began in more than 25 countries to protest against rising living costs, stagnant wages, and government spending priorities.

In Italy, nationwide strikes organized by workers and students marched through Rome to demand extra education funding while protesting stagnant compensation and military budgets, Euronews stated. Deutsche Welle reported that thousands of young protesters in Germany also mobilized over education, climate, and affordability issues, with similar mass rallies documented in Spain, France 24 reported.
