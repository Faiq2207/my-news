Government Lowers Petrol and Diesel Prices in Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 4:36 AM PKT

92 News and Aaj News reported that the government announced new, reduced prices for petrol and diesel. ARY News reported that petrol and diesel prices were revised for the next 24 hours.

According to Dawn, the price of diesel dropped below Rs350 after 4 months, while the rate of petrol was cut by Rs4.
