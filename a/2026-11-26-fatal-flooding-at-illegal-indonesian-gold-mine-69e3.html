Fatal Flooding at Illegal Indonesian Gold Mine

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 5:23 PM PKT

According to Al Jazeera and BBC News, rescue workers recovered bodies after heavy rain triggered a flood at an illegal gold mine in West Java, Indonesia. At least 13 miners died in the disaster, and another 13 remain missing.

BBC News and Al Jazeera noted that rescue efforts have been severely hindered by tough conditions, including narrow, deep, and slippery excavation pits with minimal lighting.
