President Zardari Meets Political Leaders to Discuss National Situation

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 1:53 AM PKT

According to Samaa, President Zardari held a meeting to discuss the political situation with the Deputy Prime Minister and the National Assembly Speaker. The Express Tribune reported that President Zardari met with Federal Minister Ishaq Dar and NA Speaker Ayaz Sadiq.

The Express Tribune noted that this political consultation took place against the backdrop of ongoing debates regarding proposed constitutional amendments and the creation of new provinces.
