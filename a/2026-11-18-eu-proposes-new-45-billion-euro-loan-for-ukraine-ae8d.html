EU Proposes New 45 Billion Euro Loan for Ukraine Repaid by Russia

Latest News Updates news desk, 18 November 2026, 4:34 PM PKT

According to Euronews and France 24, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen announced a proposed 45 billion euro loan to Ukraine for 2027 to cover approximately two-thirds of Kyiv's financing needs.

France 24 reported that the loan will be repaid using future war reparations from Russia, though Euronews noted that soaring borrowing costs could make the new credit financially difficult and politically complex.
