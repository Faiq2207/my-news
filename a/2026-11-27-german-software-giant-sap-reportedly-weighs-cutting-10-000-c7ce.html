German Software Giant SAP Reportedly Weighs Cutting 10,000 Jobs

Latest News Updates news desk, 27 November 2026, 5:10 PM PKT

According to CNBC and Euronews, German software major SAP is reportedly considering cutting at least 10,000 jobs as it reshapes its software business around artificial intelligence. Euronews added that if confirmed, these cuts could affect about 9% of the company's global workforce.

Following the reports, Euronews noted that SAP's shares in Frankfurt were down on Friday morning.
