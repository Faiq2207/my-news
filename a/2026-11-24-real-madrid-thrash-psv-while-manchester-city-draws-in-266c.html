Real Madrid Thrash PSV While Manchester City Draws in Champions League

Latest News Updates news desk, 25 November 2026, 4:53 AM PKT

According to France 24 and Al Jazeera, Real Madrid extended their winning run to 10 games in all competitions by routing PSV Eindhoven 5-1 in a Champions League comeback win. Jude Bellingham scored a spectacular goal and Kylian Mbappé netted twice after an early deficit.

France 24 and BBC News reported that in the same competition, Manchester City was held to a draw by Napoli after twice coming from behind, with BBC News describing City's slick footballing machine as grinding to a halt.
