French Politician Édouard Philippe Unveils Conservative Agenda

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to Euronews and France 24, former French Prime Minister Édouard Philippe has unveiled a conservative presidential agenda aimed at averting economic catastrophe in France. His proposals include slashing public spending and scrapping the statutory 35-hour workweek.

France 24 reported that Philippe also pledged to raise the country's retirement age from the current 62.9 years to 65 as part of his campaign platform.
