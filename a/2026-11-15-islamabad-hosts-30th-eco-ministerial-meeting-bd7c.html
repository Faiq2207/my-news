Islamabad Hosts 30th ECO Ministerial Meeting

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

Foreign dignitaries and official delegations have arrived in Islamabad as the capital prepares to host the two-day 30th meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Council of Ministers, according to reports by Dawn and the Express Tribune. Among those arriving is Turkish Deputy Foreign Minister Musa Kulaklikaya, who was received in the capital.

According to Dunya News and Aaj News, the foreign ministers' meeting is set to begin in Islamabad. The event is being chaired by Foreign Minister Dar, as reported by the Express Tribune and other outlets.
