Spain Faces First General Strike in 14 Years Over Housing Crisis

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

Workers across Spain have commenced the country's first general strike in 14 years, as reported by DW, France 24, and Al Jazeera. The strike has disrupted transportation networks and business activities across the country just weeks ahead of the scheduled November 29 elections.

The national action has been driven by mounting anger over soaring rents and a worsening housing crunch, according to DW and France 24. Protesters have raised concerns about being compelled into cramped rentals, with Al Jazeera citing individuals paying $500 for spaces as small as 5 square metres.
