Prague Protests Target Czech Media Funding Changes

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:44 AM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, tens of thousands of demonstrators gathered in Prague to protest government proposals to replace public broadcasting licence fees with direct state funding.

France 24 reported that critics and organizers warned the changes would harm the independence and operational viability of public media organizations.
