Anthropic Faces Market Sell-Off Following Record $81 Billion IPO

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:15 AM PKT

Artificial intelligence developer Anthropic pulled off the largest initial public offering on record, raising $81 billion, according to CNBC. However, following its blockbuster debut, the company quickly faced market turbulence, sharp stock declines, and intense anti-AI market sentiment.

BBC News and Al Jazeera reported that Anthropic's falling share prices coincided with a broader slide in technology stocks and financial markets. CNBC added that the sell-off followed pressures throughout the sector, including an ongoing crisis at Nvidia, whose stock plunged 14% to complete its longest weekly losing streak since the 2008 financial crisis.
