US and Iran Exchange Conflicting Signals on War and Negotiations

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 3:33 PM PKT

United States President Donald Trump stated that the war with Iran is effectively over and described additional strikes as quite unlikely, according to Euronews and BBC News. Speaking following discussions with Qatar's prime minister, Trump maintained that the United States remains locked and loaded should circumstances alter, while CNBC quoted him noting that Iran is militarily spent after losing its navy and enduring rampant inflation.

However, Iranian officials rejected assertions that diplomatic discussions are underway. Euronews reported that Iranian security chief Mohsen Rezaei accused Washington of producing fake news concerning imaginary negotiations. This comes amid wider diplomatic maneuvering, with Al Jazeera noting that Trump perceived positive momentum toward concluding the conflict following a Qatari ceasefire initiative.
