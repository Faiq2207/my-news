US Soldier Arrested in Britain Over Alleged Russian Espionage

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

Fox News, Al Jazeera, and France 24 reported that a senior US Army soldier was arrested in the United Kingdom on suspicion of spying for Russia. According to France 24 and DW, the 41-year-old man was detained by police near Gloucester in west England.

Al Jazeera added that London Metropolitan Police stated counterterrorism forces worked closely with the FBI and the US military to make the arrest, which Fox News noted drew a sharp warning from FBI Director Kash Patel.
