Pakistan Appoints Moazzam Ali as Diplomatic Envoy to India

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:40 PM PKT

Pakistan has moved to assign a new head of mission to its diplomatic post in New Delhi. Samaa and ARY News reported that Pakistan has decided to appoint Moazzam Ali as its new chargé d'affaires in India, while Dunya News similarly noted that he is likely to take up the role.

According to Aaj News, Moazzam Ali will be stepping in to replace Saad Warraich as the country's chargé d'affaires in India.
