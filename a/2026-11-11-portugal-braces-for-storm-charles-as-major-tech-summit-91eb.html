Portugal Braces for Storm Charles as Major Tech Summit Closes

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

Portuguese officials have placed the country on high alert and ordered the closure of schools, public services, and the Web Summit tech conference on Wednesday as Storm Charles approaches, according to France 24.

Organizers cancelled the summit's second day following warnings of tidal floods and significant disruption to transit systems. France 24 added that the tempest was forecast to reach peak intensity on Wednesday afternoon, packing winds blowing at speeds up to 120 km/h.
