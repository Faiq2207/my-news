Pakistan Hosts 30th ECO Council of Ministers Meeting in Islamabad

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

Foreign dignitaries arrived in Islamabad on Sunday as Pakistan commenced hosting the two-day 30th meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Council of Ministers, according to reports from Dawn, ARY News, and 92 News.

Dawn noted that the ECO member countries currently include Pakistan, Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, Tajikistan, Iran, Turkiye, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, the Kyrgyz Republic, and Uzbekistan. Additionally, Dunya News reported that Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar announced Pakistan will host the ECO summit in 2027.
