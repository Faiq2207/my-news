Controversy Surrounds Reported Arrest of JUI Leaders in Punjab

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 4:36 AM PKT

Geo News reported that JUI-F claimed senior leaders Abdul Ghafoor Haideri and Amjad Khan were arrested in Khushab. ARY News also reported that a JUI leader stated Haideri was unreachable amid reports of his arrest.

However, The Express Tribune reported that while JUI alleged the arrest of Maulana Ghafoor Haideri and other party leaders by Punjab Police, the Punjab Police denied that any party leader had been arrested.
