Driver Detained Over Deadly Cairo Tanker Fire

Latest News Updates news desk, 27 November 2026, 4:48 AM PKT

Egyptian prosecutors have detained a 21-year-old car driver in connection with a major crash and blaze on the Ring Road in Cairo that left 31 people dead, according to Al Jazeera, Sky News, France 24, and Fox News.

The fatal inferno was sparked when a fuel tanker overturned on the highway. Al Jazeera reported that prosecutors ordered drug testing for the detained suspect, who maintained that he was in the process of overtaking when the accident took place.
