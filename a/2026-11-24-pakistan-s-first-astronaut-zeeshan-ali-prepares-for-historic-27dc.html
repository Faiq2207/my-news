Pakistan's First Astronaut Zeeshan Ali Prepares for Historic Space Mission

Latest News Updates news desk, 25 November 2026, 4:53 AM PKT

Forty-year-old pilot Zeeshan Ali is set to become Pakistan's first astronaut to enter orbit as part of the three-member crew of China's Shenzhou-24 space mission, according to Dawn. Geo News reported that he received a send-off ahead of the historic spaceflight.

Dawn reported that the spacecraft will use a Long March-2F rocket and its launch is scheduled for 8:18am Beijing Time, which is 5:18am Pakistan Time.
