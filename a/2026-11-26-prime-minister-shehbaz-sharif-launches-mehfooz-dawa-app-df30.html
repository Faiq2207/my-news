Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif Launches Mehfooz Dawa App

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 5:23 PM PKT

According to Dawn and Samaa, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif launched the "Mehfooz Dawa" app on Thursday to prevent the circulation of counterfeit and substandard medicines in Pakistan. The prime minister described the software as a major milestone designed to help restore public confidence in the national healthcare system.

ARY News also reported on the nationwide launch of the application, emphasizing its role in curbing fake medicines across the country.
