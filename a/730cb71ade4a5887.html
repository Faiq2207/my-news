Donald Trump Nominates Natalie Baker as US Ambassador to Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 3:33 PM PKT

United States President Donald Trump has chosen Natalie Baker to be the next US envoy to Pakistan, according to reporting by ARY News, Geo News, Samaa, and Dawn. Baker currently serves as the charge d’affaires at the US embassy located in Islamabad.

Dawn reported that the official announcement came via a statement issued by the White House. The formal nomination has now been sent to the US Senate, where it will be taken up and reviewed by the foreign relations committee.
