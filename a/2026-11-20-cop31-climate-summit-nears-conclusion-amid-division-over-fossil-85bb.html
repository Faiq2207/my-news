COP31 climate summit nears conclusion amid division over fossil fuels

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to DW and Euronews, the COP31 climate summit has entered its final phase with no consensus in sight among the nearly 200 participating nations. A newly unveiled draft agreement completely omitted any mention of fossil fuels, drawing sharp criticism from environmental campaign groups.

Euronews reported that one participating country declared on the final day that it would refuse to lend its name to complacent or ineffective language within the final consensus pact.
