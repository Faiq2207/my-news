President Zardari and PM Shehbaz Agree to Resolve Matters Through Dialogue

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 3:39 AM PKT

According to reports from Dunya News and Samaa, President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif have decided that all matters will be resolved through dialogue. The Presidency announced this decision on Thursday following a meeting between the two leaders in Islamabad, as reported by Dawn.

The political discussion follows recent remarks made by PPP Chairman Bilawal. Both leaders emphasized a cooperative approach to handling current political challenges.
