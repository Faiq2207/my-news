Canada and China Agree to Ease Tariffs at APEC Summit

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:46 PM PKT

According to France 24, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and Chinese officials at the APEC summit in Shenzhen agreed to further reduce tariffs on a range of products as part of efforts to showcase international trade.
