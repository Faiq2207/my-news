Trilateral Shenzhen summit draws global attention and mixed reactions

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to France 24, the presidents of the United States, China, and Russia convened for a historic 90-minute trilateral summit in Shenzhen. Analysts noted that Russian President Vladimir Putin was particularly positioned alongside his global counterparts during the discussions.

CNBC and Euronews reported that while commentators viewed the encounter as a major diplomatic standing win for Moscow, former U.S. National Security Advisor John Bolton described the gathering merely as a photo-op that ultimately benefited the Chinese and Russian sides.
