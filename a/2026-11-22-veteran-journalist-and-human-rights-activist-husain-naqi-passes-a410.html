Veteran Journalist and Human Rights Activist Husain Naqi Passes Away in Lahore

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 4:15 PM PKT

According to Dawn and Express Tribune, veteran journalist and human rights activist Husain Naqi passed away in Lahore on Sunday. He was a former editor of The News and had been associated with the Human Rights Commission of Pakistan.

Dawn reported that an HRCP official named Muhammad Ilyas confirmed Naqi died of a cardiac arrest. Tributes for the late journalist also poured in from President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, as noted by Express Tribune.
