Sugarcane Cultivation Expands by 16 Percent in Punjab

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

Sugarcane cultivation across the province of Punjab has seen a 16 percent increase this year, according to reports published by both 92 News and Dunya News.

The rise in agricultural coverage comes directly as the annual sugarcane crushing season commences in the province, 92 News reported.
