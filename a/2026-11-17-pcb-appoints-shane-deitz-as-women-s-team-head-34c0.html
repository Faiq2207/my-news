PCB Appoints Shane Deitz as Women's Team Head Coach

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:46 PM PKT

According to Dawn and the Express Tribune, the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) appointed Australian Shane Deitz as the head coach of the national women's cricket team.

The 51-year-old coach will take charge ahead of the upcoming bilateral series against the West Indies, joining the squad in the Caribbean with a focus on future global tournaments.
