US House Republicans Halt Impeachment Effort Against Pete Hegseth

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

United States House Republicans have moved to block an impeachment push directed at Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, according to reports by DW, France 24, and NHK World. The effort, originally initiated by Republican lawmaker Thomas Massie, was shelved following a tight procedural vote, with Donald Trump praising the result, according to DW.

Al Jazeera reported that 11 Democrats were absent during the procedural ballot that halted the measure. Euronews noted that Hegseth defended his military actions in Yemen, Iran, and against alleged narcotics traffickers, affirming he stood behind each mission and characterizing the impeachment bid as an assault on American service members.
