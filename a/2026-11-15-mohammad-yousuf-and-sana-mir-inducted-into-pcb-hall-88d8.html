Mohammad Yousuf and Sana Mir Inducted into PCB Hall of Fame

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

The Pakistan Cricket Board has formally inducted former national cricket icons Mohammad Yousuf and Sana Mir into the PCB Hall of Fame, 92 News reported on Sunday.

Mohammad Yousuf stands as one of Pakistan's historic leading run-scorers in international cricket, while Sana Mir previously served as captain of the Pakistan women's national cricket team.
