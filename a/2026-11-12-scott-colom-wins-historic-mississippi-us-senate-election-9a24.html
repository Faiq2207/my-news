Scott Colom Wins Historic Mississippi US Senate Election

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 3:59 PM PKT

Democrat Scott Colom won Mississippi's US Senate contest after a prolonged vote count, defeating Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith, France 24 reported. Euronews reported that Colom achieved his victory by a margin of fewer than 5,000 votes, sealing an outcome that handed Democrats control of both chambers of Congress.

The result marks a major political milestone for the southern state. France 24 reported in separate items that Colom is the first African American to win election to the chamber from Mississippi in more than 140 years, and the state's first Black senator in 145 years, dating back to post-Civil War Reconstruction. Al Jazeera reported that Colom became the state's first Black senator since 1874.
