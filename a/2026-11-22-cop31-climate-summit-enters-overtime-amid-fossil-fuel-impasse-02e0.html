COP31 Climate Summit Enters Overtime Amid Fossil Fuel Impasse

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 4:15 PM PKT

According to Al Jazeera and DW, the UN COP31 climate summit in Turkey has entered a second day of overtime as nations struggle to reach an agreement. A newly tabled draft deal has faced severe friction because it still fails to mention fossil fuels, which remain a primary flashpoint.

Sky News reported that the president of COP31, Murat Kurum, stated that countries have not lost hope despite time rapidly running out to break the ongoing deadlock and secure a final agreement.
