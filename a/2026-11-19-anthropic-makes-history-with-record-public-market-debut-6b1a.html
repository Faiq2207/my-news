Anthropic Makes History with Record Public Market Debut

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 3:39 AM PKT

According to France 24, Anthropic has become the first pure-play artificial intelligence developer to be publicly traded on the stock market following a record-setting initial public offering. Shares opened thirty-four percent higher, briefly valuing the enterprise at over $2 trillion before closing its first trading day up seventeen percent, as reported by France 24 and Euronews. BBC News noted that the listing creates a major test for the entire industry.

Concurrently, Al Jazeera and Euronews reported that police arrested three demonstrators outside the Nasdaq exchange in New York who were warning about financial risks and an AI bubble as the company made its stock market history.
