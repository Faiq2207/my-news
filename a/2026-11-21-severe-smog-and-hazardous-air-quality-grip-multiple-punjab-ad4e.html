Severe smog and hazardous air quality grip multiple Punjab cities

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:56 PM PKT

According to Aaj News, Lahore remained the world's most polluted city with an Air Quality Index reaching 340.

Dawn reported that heavy smog pushed air quality into the hazardous category on Friday, with Lahore recording an hourly AQI reading of 465 between 7am and 8am, while Gujranwala and Multan registered readings of 661 and 641, respectively.
