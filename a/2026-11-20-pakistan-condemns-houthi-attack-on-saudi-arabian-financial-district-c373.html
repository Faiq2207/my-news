Pakistan condemns Houthi attack on Saudi Arabian financial district

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to Dawn and ARY News, Pakistan has unequivocally condemned the Houthi attack on Riyadh's tallest skyscraper, which houses the headquarters of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund.

The Foreign Office stated that the act of aggression constitutes a grave violation of the kingdom's sovereignty, as reported by Dawn.
