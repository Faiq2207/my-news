Peshawar Selected to Host PSL Opening Ceremony and First Match

Latest News Updates news desk, 24 November 2026, 7:10 AM PKT

Pakistan Cricket Board Chairman Mohsin Naqvi has announced that the opening ceremony and opening match of the 12th edition of the Pakistan Super League will take place in Peshawar, according to Dawn, Samaa, and 92 News.

Naqvi shared the decision during a press conference in Peshawar, with Aaj News noting the venue plans for the competition. Dunya News reported the event as the opening fixture of PSL 2027, while Dawn mentioned the upcoming tournament is planned for next year.
