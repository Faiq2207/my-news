Pakistan Posts Massive Total in U19 Asia Cup Final

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 3:18 PM PKT

According to Geo News, Samaa, and the Express Tribune, Pakistan won the toss and elected to bat first against India in the Under-19 Asia Cup final. Century-maker Usman Khan led the batting lineup with a powerful performance, helping build a massive total.

The Express Tribune reported that Usman and Qadir lifted Pakistan to a total of 337-9, though India fought back in the death overs by restricting the team to only 32 runs during the final five overs of the innings.
