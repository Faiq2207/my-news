Government Reduces Petrol Price by Rs2 While High-Speed Diesel Remains Unchanged

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 2:25 AM PKT

According to Dawn, Samaa, and Express Tribune, the petrol price was cut by Rs2, bringing the new price of petrol to Rs330, while the high-speed diesel price remains unchanged at Rs360.

Aaj News and ARY News noted that the reduction follows a notification issued by OGRA, marking the sixth straight time the government has slashed petrol and diesel prices.
