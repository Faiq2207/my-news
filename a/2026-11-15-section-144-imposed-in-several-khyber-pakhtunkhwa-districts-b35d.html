Section 144 Imposed in Several Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Districts

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

District authorities across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have implemented Section 144 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, banning public gatherings and various activities for a period of 30 days due to the prevailing law and order environment, according to Dawn.

Dawn initially reported the restrictions were imposed across Bajaur, Lakki Marwat, Swabi, and Tank, before later reporting that Bannu was also included among the five districts enforcing the month-long ban.
