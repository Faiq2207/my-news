Victor Wembanyama Scores 50 Points in San Antonio Spurs Win

Latest News Updates news desk, 22 November 2026, 4:15 PM PKT

According to Al Jazeera and BBC News, San Antonio Spurs star Victor Wembanyama scored 50 points and grabbed 19 rebounds to lead his team to a 114-91 victory over the New Orleans Pelicans.

Al Jazeera reported that his dominant performance fell just shy of his career-high score of 52 points, which he had previously set during a victory against Dallas on November 4.
