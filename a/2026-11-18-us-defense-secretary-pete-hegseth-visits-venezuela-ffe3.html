US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth Visits Venezuela

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to Euronews and Al Jazeera, U.S. Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth made a surprise visit to Caracas and met with officials including Delcy Rodríguez ahead of a scheduled trip to Colombia. DW reported that the visit marked the formal reestablishment of a defense partnership following years of strained bilateral ties.

Al Jazeera reported that the newly signed agreement includes provisions for joint training and intelligence sharing between the U.S. and the Venezuelan military.
