Protests and Strikes Hit Germany Amid Heated Pay Disputes

Latest News Updates news desk, 23 November 2026, 5:27 PM PKT

According to Euronews, IG Metall, Germany's largest trade union, called on more than 23,000 workers across 19 northern sites to participate in a 24-hour strike. The walkouts impacted major industrial employers, including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Siemens Gamesa, and Meyer Werft workers.

DW reported that these industrial actions coincide with heated pay disputes involving major car manufacturers and shipyards. The widespread strikes highlight growing labor tensions across the country's manufacturing sector.
