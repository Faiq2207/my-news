Musadik Malik Stresses Evidence-Based Talks with India

Latest News Updates news desk, 23 November 2026, 2:02 AM PKT

Federal minister Musadik Malik stated that any future dialogue between Pakistan and India concerning terrorism must be based on evidence, according to reports by Dawn and ARY News. Malik made these remarks while attending the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi, making him the first Pakistani minister to visit the neighboring country in years.

Samaa also noted that Malik emphasized Pakistan's readiness for bilateral talks with India during his visit, provided discussions focus on evidence rather than allegations.
