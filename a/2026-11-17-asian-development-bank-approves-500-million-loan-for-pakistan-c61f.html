Asian Development Bank Approves $500 Million Loan for Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:46 PM PKT

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) approved a $500 million results-based loan to assist Pakistan in reforming its federal civil pension system, a Manila-based lending agency announced in a statement reported by Dawn and ARY News.

According to the reports, these reforms are designed to help contain increasing fiscal pressures related to pensions and strengthen the overall pension system.
