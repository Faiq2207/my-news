Pakistan to Become Competitive Destination for Mineral Investment, Says Field Marshal Asim Munir

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 2:25 AM PKT

According to reports from Dawn and other sources, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir spoke at the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 in Islamabad. He emphasized that Pakistan is positioning itself as a competitive destination for long-term mineral investment.

In related discussions at the forum, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif directed effective measures to ensure local benefits from Reko Diq and other mineral projects, while delegations such as the Barrick Mining Corporation expressed strong interest in establishing mineral processing industries in Karachi and Gwadar, as reported by ARY News, Samaa, and 92 News.
