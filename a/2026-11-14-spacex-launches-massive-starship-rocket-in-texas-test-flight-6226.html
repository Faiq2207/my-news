SpaceX Launches Massive Starship Rocket in Texas Test Flight

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 3:18 PM PKT

According to DW and BBC News, Elon Musk's company SpaceX launched its Starship rocket on a key test flight from its launch site in Starbase, Texas. The mission included an attempt to catch both the Super Heavy booster and the upper stage spacecraft.

However, the company suffered a setback when the booster exploded over the Gulf of Mexico instead of being successfully caught at the launch site, while the upper stage spacecraft continued onward into orbit.
