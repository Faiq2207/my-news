Trump Arrives in China for APEC Summit and Trilateral Talks

Latest News Updates news desk, 18 November 2026, 4:34 PM PKT

According to CNBC and DW, US President Donald Trump has arrived in the Chinese tech hub of Shenzhen for the APEC summit. France 24 reported that he received a red carpet welcome from Xi Jinping and is set to participate in a historic trilateral meeting with Xi and Russia's Vladimir Putin.

CNBC and France 24 noted that the leaders are gathering on the sidelines of the APEC summit, where discussions will cover regional and global issues amid low expectations for major breakthroughs in the bilateral rivalry.
