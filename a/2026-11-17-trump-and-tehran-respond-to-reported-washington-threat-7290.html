Trump and Tehran Respond to Reported Washington Threat

Latest News Updates news desk, 18 November 2026, 3:51 AM PKT

President Donald Trump warned that Iran would pay a price like no other and stated that authorities take any possible threats to Washington very seriously, following intelligence reports that Iran was planning to target senior officials potentially with a radioactive bomb, as reported by Fox News, Euronews, and France 24.
