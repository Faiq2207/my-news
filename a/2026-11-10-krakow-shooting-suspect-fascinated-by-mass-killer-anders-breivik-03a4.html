Krakow Shooting Suspect Fascinated by Mass Killer Anders Breivik

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

Al Jazeera and DW reported that the suspect in a deadly November 7 shooting in Krakow was fascinated by Norway's neo-Nazi mass killer Anders Breivik.

According to Al Jazeera and DW, the 24-year-old suspect had read a significant amount of material about Breivik and other far-right figures, with speculation over his motives prompting a bitter political feud in Warsaw.
