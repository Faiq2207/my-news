Pakistan Mango Exports Drop 36pc Due to US-Iran War Disruption

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to Dawn, Pakistan's mango exports fell 36 per cent this year. Industry figures released on Wednesday indicate that the decline was driven by shipping route disruptions and rising freight costs resulting from the ongoing US-Iran war.

Data from the All Pakistan Fruit and Vegetables Exporters Association showed that the country exported a total of 78,000 tonnes of mangoes during the season.
