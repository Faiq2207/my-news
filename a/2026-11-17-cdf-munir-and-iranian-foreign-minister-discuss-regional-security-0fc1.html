CDF Munir and Iranian Foreign Minister Discuss Regional Security

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:46 PM PKT

Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir met with Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in Rawalpindi, as reported by Dawn, Geo News, and 92 News.

According to the reports, CDF Munir underscored the importance of sustained engagement, mutual trust, and close coordination between Pakistan and Iran to promote regional peace, stability, and address common security challenges.
