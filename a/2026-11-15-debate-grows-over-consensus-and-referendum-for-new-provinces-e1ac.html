Debate Grows Over Consensus and Referendum for New Provinces in Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif stated that building a consensus on creating new provinces would bring progressive change to the country's political landscape, according to Dawn, Dunya News, and ARY News. Speaking during an interview on Geo News, Asif hinted that the approaching new year could bring a new system as agreements take shape.

Meanwhile, Geo News and Samaa reported statements from Mustafa Kamal suggesting that a referendum regarding the formation of new provinces will likely take place in December.
