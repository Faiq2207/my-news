Security Operations in KP and Balochistan Leave Militants and Soldiers Dead

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 12:55 AM PKT

Dawn reported that Inter-Services Public Relations stated four troops, including a major, were martyred while 24 terrorists were killed during multiple intelligence-based operations carried out in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's North Waziristan.

Regarding the specific operations, 92 News and ARY News reported that 16 Khawarij were killed and two soldiers were martyred in North Waziristan. In an earlier account from North Waziristan, Dawn noted that 14 terrorists were killed alongside two fallen soldiers, while ARY News also reported that eight militants were neutralized and two soldiers martyred in a Balochistan operation.
