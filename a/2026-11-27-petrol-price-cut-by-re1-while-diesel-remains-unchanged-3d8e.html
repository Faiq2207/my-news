Petrol Price Cut by Re1 While Diesel Remains Unchanged

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:25 AM PKT

According to ARY News, Samaa, and Aaj News, petrol has received another price cut with new rates announced, reducing the price by Re1 per litre while the diesel rate was kept unchanged. Dawn and the Express Tribune also noted that the petrol price was reduced by Re1, with the Express Tribune specifying that petrol decreased to Rs312 per litre while HSD remained at Rs346.
