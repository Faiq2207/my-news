Pew Research Center Projects Growth for Islam and Christianity

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to Pew Research Center projections cited by Sky News and Al Jazeera, Islam is expected to surpass Christianity to become the world's largest religious group by 2035. DW reported specific figures estimating that there will be 2.57 billion Muslims and 2.52 billion Christians by that year.

Researchers attribute the shifting global religious landscape primarily to differences in fertility rates, with steady increases projected for both major faiths through 2070, as reported by Al Jazeera and DW.
