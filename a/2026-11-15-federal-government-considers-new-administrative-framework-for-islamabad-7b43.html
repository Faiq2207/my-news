Federal Government Considers New Administrative Framework for Islamabad

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

The federal government has revived plans to grant a special administrative status to the capital city of Islamabad, Samaa and ARY News reported, citing official sources.

According to Aaj News, the proposed administrative restructuring for Islamabad includes the introduction of a mayor system alongside an expansion of the city's boundaries.
