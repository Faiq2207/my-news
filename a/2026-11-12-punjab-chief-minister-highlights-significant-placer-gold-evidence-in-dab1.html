Punjab Chief Minister Highlights Significant Placer Gold Evidence in Rivers

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 3:59 PM PKT

Speaking at the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum in Islamabad, Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz announced that there is significant evidence of sufficient quantities of placer gold flowing within the five rivers of the province.

According to Dawn, the forum serves as an initiative designed to harness the mineral potential and resources of the country.
