Controversy Surrounds Russia's Return to Junior Ice Hockey Championship

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 1:38 AM PKT

Norway stated that it has been kicked out of the upcoming major ice hockey tournament to create room for Russia, DW reported. Euronews detailed that the International Ice Hockey Federation moved Norway, which is ranked 10th, down to Division 1A to free up a spot for Russia at the event in Alberta. This adjustment followed an order from the Court of Arbitration for Sport requiring the reinstatement of Russian teams.

However, Euronews reported that the alteration is not yet officially confirmed, and Russia currently has no guarantee of receiving Canadian visas. Amid the ongoing dispute over Russia's reintroduction to global hockey, Switzerland has decided to join a boycott, according to DW.
