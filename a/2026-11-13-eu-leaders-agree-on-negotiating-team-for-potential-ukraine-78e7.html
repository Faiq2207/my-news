EU Leaders Agree on Negotiating Team for Potential Ukraine Peace Talks

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 2:25 AM PKT

According to DW and Euronews, Germany, France, the UK, Italy, and Poland, alongside the EU itself and NATO, will jointly represent Europe in possible peace talks. Euronews and Al Jazeera noted that the team was chosen after months of wavering, though leaders declined to appoint a single envoy.

Meanwhile, BBC Russia Editor Steve Rosenberg noted that Moscow views the inclusion of European security in discussions as a victory, as reported by BBC News.
