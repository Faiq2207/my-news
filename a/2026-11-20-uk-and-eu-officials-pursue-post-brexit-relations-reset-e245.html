UK and EU officials pursue post-Brexit relations reset in Brussels

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to Euronews and France 24, UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham and top EU officials are gathering in Brussels for a summit aimed at resetting relations following the divisions of Brexit. The discussions are expected to pave the way for closer ties and initial agreements.

BBC News reported that the summit is anticipated to approve a youth mobility scheme, alongside new arrangements concerning food trade and carbon emissions. However, Euronews noted that negotiations regarding university fee reductions have been delayed by six months.
