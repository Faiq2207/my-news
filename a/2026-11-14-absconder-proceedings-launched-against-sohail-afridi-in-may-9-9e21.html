Absconder Proceedings Launched Against Sohail Afridi in May 9 Case

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 12:55 AM PKT

The Express Tribune reported that an anti-terrorism court in Peshawar initiated absconder proceedings against Sohail Afridi in connection with the Radio Pakistan case. The court issued Section 87 notices after police authorities reported that they failed to arrest individuals tied to violence that occurred on May 9-10.

Dunya News confirmed that proclamation proceedings were officially initiated against Sohail Afridi regarding the May 9 Radio Pakistan attack incident.
