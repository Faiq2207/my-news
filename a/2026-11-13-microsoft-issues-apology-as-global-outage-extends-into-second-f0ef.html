Microsoft Issues Apology as Global Outage Extends Into Second Day

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 2:25 AM PKT

According to BBC News, Microsoft expressed that it is deeply sorry for a global outage that caused nearly 24 hours of disruption. Al Jazeera reported that server failures stretched into a second day, impacting governments, businesses, and individuals globally.

Experts noted that the event serves as a stark reminder of the heavy global reliance on a small group of major tech providers, while workers worldwide have found humor in the disruptions to Outlook, Teams, and Copilot.
