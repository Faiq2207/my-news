Government Adjusts Fuel Prices as Petrol Falls and Diesel Rises

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:15 AM PKT

According to ARY News, Dunya News, Samaa, Dawn, and Aaj News, the oil and gas regulatory authority has revised the prices of petroleum products. The price of petrol has been reduced by Re1 per litre, while diesel prices have been increased by Rs2 per litre.

Express Tribune reported that the new price for petrol is set at Rs322, while High-Speed Diesel (HSD) stands at Rs357, with the adjustments remaining effective until Nov 23.
