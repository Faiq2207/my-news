Iraqi forces and Kataib Hezbollah reach compromise over stronghold

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Al Jazeera, the Iraqi government reached a compromise with the Iran-backed militia Kataib Hezbollah after Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi prepared security personnel to intervene against the group over alleged smuggling operations.

France 24 reported that state media confirmed security forces subsequently deployed into the Jurf al-Sakhar stronghold under the broker of Shi'ite politicians.
