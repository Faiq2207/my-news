Fuel Prices Adjusted by Regulatory Authority

Latest News Updates news desk, 13 November 2026, 2:50 AM PKT

According to Dawn and ARY News, the government reduced the price of petrol by Rs3 per litre and high-speed diesel (HSD) by Rs4 per litre, following an announcement by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA).

After the revision, petrol will retail at Rs332 per litre and high-speed diesel will cost Rs360 starting Friday.
