Deadly Highway Crash in Egypt

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 5:23 PM PKT

According to France 24 and BBC News, at least 31 people were killed in Cairo after a fuel tanker overturned on a busy highway and a large fire broke out. Egypt's public prosecutor's office stated that the suspected driver was arrested on Thursday.

Euronews reported that the deadly crash occurred on Giza's Ring Road in Waraq, leaving 31 dead and 21 injured and highlighting the country's severe road safety risks. Al Jazeera noted that the tragedy has renewed discussions regarding the safety of Egypt's roads.
