Nationwide strikes disrupt public services across European countries

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, nationwide labor strikes began in Italy, disrupting local transport, schools, and hospitals as protesters marched to demand higher wages, lower living costs, and stronger social protections.

Euronews reported that public administration workers in Portugal also initiated a national strike on the same Friday, severely impacting healthcare, education, and justice services amid similar demands for improved pay and working conditions.
