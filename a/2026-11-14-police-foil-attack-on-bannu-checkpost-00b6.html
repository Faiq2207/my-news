Police Foil Attack on Bannu Checkpost

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 3:18 PM PKT

According to Dawn and ARY News, police successfully foiled a terrorist attack on the Kingar Pul Police Checkpost in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's Bannu district late Friday night. A spokesperson for the police stated that heavily armed assailants launched a sudden strike against the post.

Security personnel retaliated swiftly, eliminating four terrorists during the engagement.
