Pakistani Leaders Hold Call with Astronaut Zeeshan Ali in Space

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:40 PM PKT

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir held a conversation on Saturday with Pakistani astronaut Zeeshan Ali, according to Dawn, Dunya News, Express Tribune, Geo News, and Samaa. Dawn reported that Ali is aboard the Shenzhou-24 mission, while Geo News specified that he is stationed on the Tiangong space station.

According to Dawn, Ali became the first Pakistani in orbit on Wednesday. Express Tribune reported that the premier and field marshal lauded the space mission as a historic achievement and praised the enduring friendship between Pakistan and China. In addition, Aaj News reported that the astronaut has commenced scientific experiments while in space.
