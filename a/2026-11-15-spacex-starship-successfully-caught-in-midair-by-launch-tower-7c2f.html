SpaceX Starship Successfully Caught in Midair by Launch Tower

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

SpaceX's Starship spacecraft was successfully caught by giant mechanical arms at its Texas launch tower following a 22-hour journey, according to Euronews. France 24 reported that the achievement marks the first time SpaceX managed to recapture its massive reusable rocketship, noting that smaller previous models relanded using heavy legs rather than full reusability.

Crowds outside the Texas Starbase cheered as the mechanical arms closed around the spaceship in a historic first that company officials said changed everything, Euronews reported. Al Jazeera noted that officials stated this catch demonstrates the rocket's full reusability, which is crucial for rapid and low-cost recycling.
