Deadly Industrial Blast Hits Turkish Steel Plant

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 1:38 AM PKT

A major explosion took place at a steel factory near Izmir in western Turkey, according to Al Jazeera and BBC News. BBC News identified the facility as the Habas Demir Celik plant in Aliaga.

Casualty counts varied slightly across reports. Al Jazeera and DW reported that at least 16 people died, citing an announcement by President Recep Tayyip Erdogan following a cabinet meeting. Euronews reported that at least 17 individuals were killed and 13 others were injured. According to Euronews and Al Jazeera, the disaster was triggered when a crane carrying a boiler collapsed into an industrial furnace.
