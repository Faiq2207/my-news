US, China, and Russia Hold Historic Trilateral Summit in Shenzhen

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 4:33 PM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, US President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping, and Russian President Vladimir Putin participated in the first-ever trilateral meeting in Shenzhen, China. CNBC reported that the discussions included nuclear weapons discussions, with Trump stating that the three nations are uniquely positioned to address reductions.

France 24 noted that the meeting lasted about an hour and 20 minutes, though critics expressed concern that it could lead to carving the globe into spheres of influence. Al Jazeera reported that Trump greeted both Putin and Xi as his friends during separate preliminary talks ahead of the main summit.
