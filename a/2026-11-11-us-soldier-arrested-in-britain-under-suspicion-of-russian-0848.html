US Soldier Arrested in Britain Under Suspicion of Russian Espionage

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

A member of the United States army has been taken into custody in the United Kingdom over allegations of espionage for Russia, London police reported according to France 24 and Euronews. The arrest on Tuesday followed a joint inquiry conducted alongside American military and law enforcement officials.

CNBC reported that the individual is 41 years old and is being held in London under the UK's National Security Act. Euronews noted that the Federal Bureau of Investigation described the case as an issue of utmost concern to Washington and allied governments.
