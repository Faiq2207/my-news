Pakistan Reports Tenth Polio Case of 2026 in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Latest News Updates news desk, 26 November 2026, 4:36 AM PKT

According to Dawn, the National Emergency Operations Centre for Polio Eradication (NEOC) confirmed the country's 10th paralytic polio case of 2026. The infection was identified in the Karak district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, representing the district's first case in 6 years.

Geo News reported that the affected patient is a 14-month-old girl, bringing the countrywide tally to 10 cases for the year.
