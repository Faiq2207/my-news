Pakistan and Portugal Deepen Bilateral Relations During High-Level Visit

Latest News Updates news desk, 24 November 2026, 7:10 AM PKT

Portuguese Minister of State and Foreign Affairs Paulo Rangel arrived in Islamabad and affirmed Lisbon's full backing for Pakistan's European Union GSP+ status, according to Dawn. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Portuguese officials also held discussions focused on bilateral trade and investment, Samaa reported.

Both nations signed a visa-free entry agreement for diplomatic passport holders, Dunya News noted. Deputy Prime Minister Dar stated that Pakistan and Portugal agreed to expand mutual cooperation across agriculture, technology, and labour mobility, according to Aaj News, while President Zardari also pursued stronger ties with Portugal.
