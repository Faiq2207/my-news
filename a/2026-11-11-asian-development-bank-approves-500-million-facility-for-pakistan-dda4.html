Asian Development Bank Approves $500 Million Facility for Pakistan

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) has approved contingent disaster financing facilities reaching up to $500 million to help Pakistan strengthen its disaster preparedness and facilitate response efforts, according to Dawn, Dunya News, and ARY News.

The Manila-based financial institution stated that the approved funds will deliver prompt budget support, enabling the nation to withstand disasters and build climate resilience, Dawn reported.
