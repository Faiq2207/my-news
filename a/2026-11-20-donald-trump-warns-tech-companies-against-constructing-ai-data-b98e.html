Donald Trump Warns Tech Companies Against Constructing AI Data Centres Abroad

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:15 AM PKT

US President Donald Trump announced that American technology firms will face a tremendous tax if they choose to build artificial intelligence data centres in foreign countries, Al Jazeera and CNBC reported. Trump made the statements during a White House press conference following his return from a trilateral summit in China with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

BBC News noted that Trump also threatened a potential ban on companies establishing AI data centres abroad, though he offered limited details and observed that members of his own team were hearing the proposal for the first time. The warning comes amid broader market developments, as CNBC reported that US data centre construction costs rose 13% in 2026 due to soaring labor and power demands.
