Senegal Reinstated as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations Winners

Latest News Updates news desk, 25 November 2026, 4:53 AM PKT

According to France 24 and BBC News, Senegal were reinstated as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners after the sport's highest court upheld their appeal. Senegal contested the Confederation of African Football's decision to strip them of the title and declare Morocco champions following a chaotic final.

France 24 noted experts viewed the ruling as a positive precedent in sports law, while Euronews reported that Morocco has vowed to fight on and raised questions over alleged contact between a judge and the Senegalese federation.
