President Zardari Holds Discussions with Chaudhry Shujaat in Lahore

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:25 AM PKT

According to 92 News, President Zardari arrived in Lahore on a two-day visit. Samaa and the Express Tribune reported that President Zardari met with PML-Q chief Chaudhry Shujaat Hussain at his Lahore residence to discuss national affairs and the country's political situation.

Dawn added that the Presidency released a statement confirming the meeting took place at Shujaat's residence, where Zardari also enquired after the former prime minister's health and wished him a speedy recovery.
