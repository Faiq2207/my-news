Surge of Migrant Arrivals Reaches Spain's Canary Islands

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 1:23 AM PKT

A major wave of arrivals reached Spain's Canary Islands this weekend. France 24 reported that nearly 2,200 people arrived by boat across 48 hours, while DW reported that nearly 1,600 migrants landed on Saturday alone, predominantly from Senegal, Gambia, and Guinea. Al Jazeera reported that nearly 1,500 people arrived in a single day.

Euronews reported that 1,488 individuals arrived across the islands in one day, including 12 cayucos carrying 1,235 people to El Hierro, alongside boats reaching Gran Canaria and Tenerife. Regional President Clavijo stated that local authorities are overwhelmed, while Euronews noted that two-day arrivals exceeded 2,300.

According to France 24, the Spanish Interior Ministry reported that more than 13,000 migrants had arrived on the islands between January and the conclusion of last month.
