French Interior Ministry Official Relieved of Post Over Leaks Probe

Latest News Updates news desk, 23 November 2026, 2:02 AM PKT

Euronews reported that a top official at the French interior ministry asked to be relieved of his post after being suspected of informing the National Rally about probes into the party. The official condemned the situation as a 'manhunt'.

According to France 24, far-right leader Marine Le Pen stated on Sunday that she did not receive, nor ask for, confidential information on the investigation from senior official Hugues Moutouh, though she could not confirm if other party members benefited.
