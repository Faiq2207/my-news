Pakistan defeats Sri Lanka to win Test series 1-0

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 4:29 PM PKT

According to Dunya News, Dawn, and Geo News, Pakistan defeated Sri Lanka by eight wickets in the second and final Test match to seal the series 1-0 on Friday.

Dawn and Express Tribune reported that the hosts successfully chased down a target of 90 runs in 13 overs on the fourth morning, with contributions from players like Aamer Jamal and Azan Awais.
