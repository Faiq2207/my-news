UEFA Backs Philippe Diallo to Challenge FIFA Presidency

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:44 AM PKT

According to France 24 and Euronews, European football's governing body UEFA announced its unanimous backing for French Football Federation chief Philippe Diallo to run in the upcoming FIFA presidential election.

DW and Al Jazeera noted that Diallo will challenge incumbent Gianni Infantino, joining critics who have voiced strong opposition to the current FIFA leadership.
