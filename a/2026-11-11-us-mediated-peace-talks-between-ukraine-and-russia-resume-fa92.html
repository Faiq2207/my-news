US-Mediated Peace Talks Between Ukraine and Russia Resume in Abu Dhabi

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:51 PM PKT

Russia and Ukraine have resumed peace negotiations in Abu Dhabi for a second day under United States mediation, according to reports from France 24 and Euronews. Kyiv's chief negotiator Rustem Umerov confirmed the continuation of discussions on Wednesday, while Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated he was happy with shifts in the American mediators' approach.

Major points of discussion include territory, security arrangements, and ceasefire terms, Euronews reported. The first day was described by Kyiv as constructive, with prisoner exchanges and security guarantees addressed, though Euronews noted a full ceasefire is not considered close. Meanwhile, Al Jazeera highlighted that US President Donald Trump commented that post-war Ukraine would have a different shape.
