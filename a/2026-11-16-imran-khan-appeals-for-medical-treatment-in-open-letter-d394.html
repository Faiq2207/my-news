Imran Khan Appeals for Medical Treatment in Open Letter

Latest News Updates news desk, 16 November 2026, 4:08 PM PKT

According to Dawn, incarcerated former prime minister and PTI founder Imran Khan appealed for medical treatment for himself and his wife, Bushra Bibi, in an open letter addressed to the chief justices of the Federal Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, and the Islamabad High Court.

Aaj News reported that Khan claimed in his letter that he faces the risk of a heart attack and a brain haemorrhage.
