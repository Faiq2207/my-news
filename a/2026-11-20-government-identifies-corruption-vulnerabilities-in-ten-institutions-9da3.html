Government Identifies Corruption Vulnerabilities in Ten Institutions

Latest News Updates news desk, 21 November 2026, 3:15 AM PKT

According to Dawn and Samaa, Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar announced that the federal government has identified vulnerabilities and weaknesses creating corruption opportunities across ten institutions through a self-audit survey.

The initiative was described as a step toward undertaking comprehensive self-accountability within government bodies.
