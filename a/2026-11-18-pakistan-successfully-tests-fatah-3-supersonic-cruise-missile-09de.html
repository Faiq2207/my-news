Pakistan Successfully Tests Fatah-3 Supersonic Cruise Missile

Latest News Updates news desk, 19 November 2026, 3:35 AM PKT

According to Dawn and Aaj News, the Pakistan Army successfully carried out the test launch of the Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile on Wednesday. ARY News noted that this achievement marks a new milestone for the country's military development.

The military's media wing, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), announced the successful test in an official statement, highlighting the advancement in the nation's defense capabilities.
