Makkah Pact Nations Convene in Islamabad to Address Regional Conflict

Latest News Updates news desk, 27 November 2026, 4:48 AM PKT

Officials from the mutual defence arrangement between Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Turkey are convening in Islamabad for a trilateral meeting. GNN reported that the Mecca alliance meeting is scheduled to take place in the federal capital on November 27-28.

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif stated that the conflict between Saudi Arabia and Houthi fighters in Yemen will be addressed during the talks, according to Dawn and Geo News. Khawaja Asif remarked that the persistent instability south of Saudi Arabia will naturally be among the topics of discussion. Aaj News added that the member nations of the Makkah pact will focus directly on the Yemen situation.
