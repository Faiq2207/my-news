Russian Ambassador to the United States Alexander Darchiev Dies Suddenly at Age 66

Latest News Updates news desk, 12 November 2026, 2:55 AM PKT

According to Fox News, Euronews, and Al Jazeera, Russia's ambassador to the United States, Alexander Darchiev, has died suddenly at the age of 66. Moscow's foreign ministry announced the veteran diplomat's sudden passing without providing further details.

Fox News reported that Darchiev had been appointed by President Vladimir Putin to lead efforts toward a rapprochement with the administration of US President Donald Trump.
