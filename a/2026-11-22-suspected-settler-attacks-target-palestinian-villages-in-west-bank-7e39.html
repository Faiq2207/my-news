Suspected Settler Attacks Target Palestinian Villages in West Bank

Latest News Updates news desk, 23 November 2026, 2:02 AM PKT

According to Euronews and France 24, Israeli authorities opened an investigation after Palestinian vehicles and properties were vandalised and set on fire in four villages in the occupied West Bank on Sunday, in attacks local activists blamed on Israeli settlers.

Al Jazeera and DW reported that Israeli opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot condemned the series of arson attacks as 'Jewish terrorism'. Local residents and activists noted that around eight to twelve vehicles were torched and racist anti-Arab graffiti was discovered.
