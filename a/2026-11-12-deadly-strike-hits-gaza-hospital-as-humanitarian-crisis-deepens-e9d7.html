Deadly Strike Hits Gaza Hospital as Humanitarian Crisis Deepens

Latest News Updates news desk, 13 November 2026, 2:50 AM PKT

An overnight Israeli air strike on Nasser Hospital in southern Gaza killed at least six Palestinians, including a teenage boy and a surgeon, according to health officials cited by France 24 and Al Jazeera. Al Jazeera reported it was the seventh time Israel has targeted the hospital.

Health officials and the World Health Organization stated that the strike injured at least 27 others and marked the deadliest single strike since Israel's elections last month, according to France 24 and Euronews. Meanwhile, Al Jazeera reported that a baby died from hypothermia overnight in a family tent.
