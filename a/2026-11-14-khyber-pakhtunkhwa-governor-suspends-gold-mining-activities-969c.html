Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Suspends Gold Mining Activities

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 12:55 AM PKT

Dawn reported that Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi announced a ban on gold mining across the province, alongside the revocation of every mining lease, in an effort to curb illegal mining practices. The announcement was made during a press conference in Peshawar.

According to Dawn, Governor Kundi also condemned an attack in Kohat. The provincial suspension of all gold extraction operations was additionally confirmed by reports from 92 News and Aaj News.
