Nottingham Forest Defeats Chelsea as Gibbs-White Shines

Latest News Updates news desk, 28 November 2026, 4:25 AM PKT

BBC News reported that Nottingham Forest defeated a struggling Chelsea side 3-2 at the City Ground for the first time since 1997. Morgan Gibbs-White scored two goals, while Igor Jesus contributed a stunning volley.

Following the match, BBC News noted that Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso called for togetherness after his team fell to their third consecutive defeat. Meanwhile, Nottingham Forest head coach Oliver Glasner praised Igor Jesus's performance, and defender Wesley Fofana was booed by his own fans.
