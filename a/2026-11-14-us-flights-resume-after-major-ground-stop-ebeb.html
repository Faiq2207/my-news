US Flights Resume After Major Ground Stop

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 12:55 AM PKT

According to France 24 and the BBC, a failure in aging telecommunications equipment caused a nationwide flight suspension in the US, leading to the longest FAA ground stop since September 11, 2001. CNBC and Al Jazeera reported that US Transport Secretary Sean Duffy stated the agency is working to find out whether the chaos was caused by incompetence or faulty equipment and that they are going to hold people accountable.

France 24 noted that more than 8,200 flights, or over 31% of those scheduled, were canceled during the nearly five-hour ground stoppage. Transport Secretary Sean Duffy also asked Congress for $30 bn to upgrade aviation infrastructure, while Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin stated that officials have found no evidence so far of a cyberattack, though they are not ruling it out, according to France 24.
