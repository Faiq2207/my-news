Pope Leo XIV Concludes South American Tour

Latest News Updates news desk, 17 November 2026, 4:44 AM PKT

According to Euronews and France 24, Pope Leo XIV concluded his 12-day tour spanning Uruguay, Argentina, and Peru, shortly after undergoing lung cancer surgery in Rome.

Euronews and Fox News reported that during a farewell Mass in Lima, Peru, the pontiff was struck near the eye by a flying rosary, though he remained uninjured and continued his schedule.
