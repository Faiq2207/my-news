France Accuses Iran of Cyberattacks on Supermarket Refrigerators

Latest News Updates news desk, 10 November 2026, 3:33 PM PKT

The French government has publicly accused the cyber unit of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps of conducting an unprecedented cyber campaign against supermarket refrigeration units, according to CNBC and France 24. The October sabotage operations caused millions of euros in damage across hundreds of retail stores, CNBC reported.

French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot confirmed the findings and stated that France will pursue coordinated European retaliatory measures and sanctions against Tehran, according to France 24 and Al Jazeera. The incident was also acknowledged in reporting by NHK World.
