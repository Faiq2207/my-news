Gadi Eisenkot Reaches Preliminary Coalition Deal in Israel

Latest News Updates news desk, 27 November 2026, 5:10 PM PKT

According to France 24, Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party and a left-wing faction agreed on a preliminary coalition deal on Thursday. The agreement followed a brief dispute that had posed a challenge to his bid to unseat Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu following an inconclusive election last month.

France 24 further reported that the coalition deal was struck with Yair Golan's left-leaning Democrats, bringing the country's opposition closer to forming a government after no single bloc secured a majority in the October elections.
