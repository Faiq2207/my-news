Islamabad High Court Suspends Upcoming Local Government Elections

Latest News Updates news desk, 14 November 2026, 2:25 AM PKT

According to Dawn, the Islamabad High Court suspended the upcoming local government elections in the federal capital on Friday. The decision puts the Election Commission of Pakistan's latest election schedule on hold until the next hearing.

The suspension follows a writ petition filed last week by Chaudhry Amjad Ali.
