Pablo Hernandez de Cos Appointed to Lead European Central Bank

Latest News Updates news desk, 27 November 2026, 4:48 AM PKT

European leaders meeting in Brussels have agreed to nominate Spanish economist Pablo Hernandez de Cos as the next president of the European Central Bank, according to reports by France 24, Al Jazeera, CNBC, and Euronews. Hernandez de Cos, the former head of the Bank of Spain, will succeed outgoing chief Christine Lagarde.

France 24 noted that the appointment comes as Europe's primary financial institution faces challenges that include high borrowing costs, government debt, and a resurgence in inflation tied to the war involving Iran. Euronews added that the institution oversees interest rates across the 21 nations that use the euro currency.
