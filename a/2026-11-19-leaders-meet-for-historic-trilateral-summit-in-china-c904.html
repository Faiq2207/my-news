Leaders Meet for Historic Trilateral Summit in China

Latest News Updates news desk, 20 November 2026, 3:39 AM PKT

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping, and Vladimir Putin issued a joint peace declaration following their first-ever trilateral summit on the sidelines of the APEC meeting in China, Fox News, BBC News, and Euronews reported. Chinese President Xi urged the US and Russia to join forces to safeguard world peace, according to Al Jazeera.

DW noted that while Trump called the participants history-makers, Xi described the discussions as fruitful and Putin termed them open and candid. However, European leaders meeting in Berlin rejected any peace format akin to past arrangements, with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz stating that a Yalta format will not bring peace, as reported by Euronews.
