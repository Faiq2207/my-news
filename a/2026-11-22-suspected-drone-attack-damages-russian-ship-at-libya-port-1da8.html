Suspected Drone Attack Damages Russian Ship at Libya Port

Latest News Updates news desk, 23 November 2026, 2:02 AM PKT

According to local officials reported by France 24, a Russian navy corvette docked at the Benghazi naval base in eastern Libya was hit on Sunday by a suspected Ukrainian sea drone. BBC News and other sources noted that Libya's eastern government stated the ship had stopped for fuel and supplies on its way back to Russia.

Euronews reported that Russian authorities began towing a damaged military-linked cargo vessel following a suspected Ukrainian drone attack near Finnish waters. Al Jazeera and DW confirmed that a Russian vessel was hit at Libya's Benghazi naval base in what is suspected to be a drone strike, raising regional security concerns.
