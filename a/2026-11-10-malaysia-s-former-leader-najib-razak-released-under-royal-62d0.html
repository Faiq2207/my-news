Malaysia's Former Leader Najib Razak Released Under Royal Pardon

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

DW and BBC News reported that former Malaysian leader Najib Razak has left prison under a royal pardon. DW noted that his release prompted a minister to resign in protest.

According to DW and BBC News, Najib Razak still faces a separate 15-year prison sentence over his role in the multi-billion-dollar corruption scandal involving the 1MDB sovereign wealth fund.
