DLR, NASA, and Canada Plan Greenhouse Concept for the Moon

Latest News Updates news desk, 11 November 2026, 3:30 AM PKT

Euronews reported that the German Aerospace Center (DLR), together with NASA and the Canadian Space Agency (CSA) or Canada's CSA, is developing a concept for greenhouses on the Moon.

Euronews described the initiative as a concrete research project aimed at growing vegetables and food for astronauts in lunar environments.
