Israel Confirms Death of Senior Hamas Commander in Gaza Strike

Latest News Updates news desk, 15 November 2026, 3:47 PM PKT

According to BBC News, both Hamas and IDF officials confirmed the death of Samir Abu Younis, described as the commander of the Rafah Brigade. Al Jazeera reported that the Israeli airstrike that killed the senior commander took place on Saturday in Khan Younis.

Sky News noted that the commander had previously helped plan and oversee the attack on October 7, 2023.
